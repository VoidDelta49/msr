# Decoding eleven Philippine bikes for faithful 3D models

The eleven bikes can be modelled convincingly from the current record, but only after one decision per bike (which generation) and a short set of photo checks. The evidence is strongest for the **Kawasaki Ninja H2**, whose dimensions and hardware come straight from Kawasaki USA spec sheets ([Kawasaki USA](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs)), and for the 2025-26 scooters, where Philippine press and dealer pages agree to within a millimetre or two. It is weakest for the **Kawasaki Rouser NS125 Fi** (no Philippine dimensions exist; the Bajaj Pulsar NS125 is the proxy), the **Rusi Macho 125** (no length, width or height anywhere; one side-on photo is the whole visual record) and the **Suzuki Smash Fi** (no dimensions at all). The recommended targets are the **2026 Honda Click125** (2022 body plus the February 2026 facelift), the **2025 all-new Yamaha Aerox**, the **2025 all-new NMAX**, the **2025 XMAX 300**, the **MY2026 Suzuki Raider R150**, the **Smash Fi** on the Smash 115 body, the **2025 Ninja H2**, the **2024-restyle CBR650R**, the **third-generation (2018) TMX Supremo** and the **Macho TC 125**. The scale runs from a 1,919 mm Click to a 2,180 mm XMAX, with wheels from 13 inches (NMAX) to 18 inches (TMX). Every official colour name below is sourced, but every hex value is an estimate from the name and press description, not sampled from a swatch. Research ran on 2026-11-06 without access to any manufacturer product page (Honda PH, Yamaha PH, Suzuki PH, Kawasaki PH and Rusi sites all failed or were blocked), and no photo was viewed except one Macho image, so every shape description comes from text. The first verification priorities are the Click body generation, Macho second and third views, the Rouser's real body, and the CBR650R's Philippine specification.

Three relationships help a modeller sanity-check proportions. The Aerox is **61 mm longer, 23 mm wider, 95 mm taller and 70 mm longer in wheelbase** than the 2026 Click, with a 140 mm rear tyre against the Click's 100 mm, while the XMAX is **245 mm longer, 55 mm wider, 210 mm taller (screen down) and 200 mm longer in wheelbase** than the NMAX, with a 15-inch front wheel that visibly outsizes its 14-inch rear, whereas the NMAX sits on equal 13-inch wheels ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/honda-click-125-2026-ph-prices-specs-a5361-20260208), [ZigWheels PH](https://zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/yamaha/mio-aerox/specifications), [Carmudi XMAX](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/refreshed-yamaha-xmax-now-in-ph), [Carmudi NMAX](https://carmudi.com.ph/journal/yamaha-motor-ph-starts-all-new-nmax-deliveries-to-clients)). The Ninja H2 and CBR650R share almost the same footprint (wheelbase 1,455 versus 1,450 mm), though the H2 is 20 mm wider, 30 kg heavier and carries a 200-section rear tyre against the CBR's 180; the CBR's nominally longer 2,120 mm figure most likely reflects different measurement conventions rather than a longer bike, so scale them from wheelbase and tyre sizes, not overall length ([Kawasaki USA](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs), [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)). Finally, the Raider and Click share a 1,280 mm wheelbase but differ by 95 mm in height, and the Smash (1,240 mm) is taller than the Raider (1,040 versus 980 mm), so the two Suzuki underbones are distinguished by stance and seat, not by length ([Top Gear PH Raider](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/suzuki-raider-r150-2026-launch-a5361-20251117), [suzukicycles.org](https://suzukicycles.org/Smash/Smash-Fi-115.shtml)).

## Scooters: three Yamaha facelifts and one Honda reset reshape the step-through class

### Honda Click: low, angular and, since February 2026, vented

Honda Philippines sells two bodies under one name, and the choice drives every other detail. The **Click125i** launched in September 2018 with what Top Gear PH called an "almost alien-esque front end" and 80/90-14 and 90/90-14 tyres ([Top Gear PH 2018](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/honda-unveils-click-125i-click-150i-a2634-20180922), [Carmudi](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/click-125i/specifications)). The **all-new Click125** of December 2022 brought a redesigned front and body, new decals, all-LED lighting, new black cast wheels, wider tubeless tyres and a wavy-edge front disc ([Honda PH](https://www.hondaph.com/motorcycle/news/the-all-new-click125-arrives-in-the-philippines-as-the-ultimate-game-changer)). The February 2026 "Game Changer Upgrade" keeps that silhouette and adds vents or ducts in the side fairings, a redesigned grab bar, a subtle headlight redesign and a bag hook by the floorboard ([Top Gear PH news](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/honda-click-125-2026-ph-launch-a5361-20260206), [Top Gear PH feature](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/honda-click-125-2026-ph-prices-specs-a5361-20260208)). Aggregators that still show a "Click 125i 2026" merge the old body's 679 mm width and tyres with the new label, so ignore them. Use the 2026 figures in the table (687 mm wide, 1,075 mm tall).

The running gear is simple: 14-inch black cast wheels, one front disc with a wavy outer edge, a rear drum, combined braking (the left lever works both ends) and no ABS, a telescopic fork, a single rear shock on a unit swing, and a short muffler with an aluminium end cap ([Honda PH 2022](https://www.hondaph.com/motorcycle/news/the-all-new-click125-arrives-in-the-philippines-as-the-ultimate-game-changer), [Motodeal](https://motodeal.com.ph/motorcycles/honda/click-125/standard)). The cockpit is a fully digital panel, with a magnetic key integrated into the seat-eject button and a USB port in the front compartment; the 2026 Smart Edition adds a smart key, idling stop and alarm ([Top Gear PH news](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/honda-click-125-2026-ph-launch-a5361-20260206)). The 2026 Standard (PHP 82,400) comes in **Pearl Sylvestris Gray, Pearl Arctic White and Obsidian Black Metallic** as two-tone finishes, while the Smart Edition is solid matte in **Mat Axis Gray Metallic and Mat Fresco Brown** with shiny gold 3D "Click" badges ([Top Gear PH feature](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/honda-click-125-2026-ph-prices-specs-a5361-20260208)). Motodeal alone adds a "Street" trim in Matte Cyber Grape Metallic ([Motodeal](https://motodeal.com.ph/motorcycles/honda/click-125/standard)). Older sets matter if the game's bike is a 2018-2022 unit: 2022 offered Pearl Crimson Red, Stellar Blue Metallic and the grey, white and black above, and the 2021 old-body set (Vivid Blue, Valiant Red, Nitric Orange, Matte Gunpowder Black Metallic, Matte Pearl Crater White with contrasting gold stripes) ([Honda PH 2021](https://www.hondaph.com/motorcycle/news/the-new-honda-click125i-changes-the-game-again), [Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/honda-adds-matte-white-to-click-125i-colorways.html)). Recognition cues: tiny proportions, black wheels, vented fairings, the restyled grab bar and gold badges. **Confidence: dimensions High; body shape Medium (headlight, tail-lamp and grab-bar geometry are undescribed); 2026 wheel colour Medium (retained from 2022 by inference); cockpit layout Low.**

### Yamaha Aerox: a full-fairing mini-maxi that borrows XMAX styling

The all-new Aerox launched on 1 August 2025 without the "Mio" prefix, positioned in Yamaha's MAX Series, in Standard and SP trims ([ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/yamaha-motor-ph-launches-all-new-aerox), [Carmudi](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/all-new-yamaha-aerox-now-in-ph)). It keeps the previous generation's silhouette but sharpens the details: an extended front fascia that ZigWheels compares to the XMAX, an extended angular headlamp housing, broad sculpted front fairings, a short smoked windscreen, a tapered rear with a pointed LED tail lamp, and turn signals that protrude on each side ([ZigWheels PH first look](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-feature-stories/all-new-yamaha-aerox-first-look), [ZigWheels PH reasons](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-feature-stories/yamaha-aerox-top-four-reasons-to-buy)). Two sources call the headlamps "dual", so a twin-element cluster under one housing is the best-supported reading, though no source gives the geometry. The Standard carries a forged-carbon-look trim under the headlight and on the inner fairings; the SP swaps these for red accents, adds an "SP" badge on the outer fairings, red seat stitching with "Aerox" script and **red-painted wheels** ([ZigWheels PH colours](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-feature-stories/here-are-the-all-new-yamaha-aeroxs-available-colors)).

Hardware is 14-inch cast wheels at both ends, 230 mm discs front and rear (the rear disc is new), a fork with 30 mm tubes, and a swing-unit rear described as having two shocks and a sub-tank; the SP has single-channel ABS ([ZigWheels PH first look](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-feature-stories/all-new-yamaha-aerox-first-look), [Inside Racing](https://insideracing.com.ph/?p=86957)). The rear stance is heavy because of the 140/70-14 tyre and 145 mm clearance. The cockpit is R-Series inspired with a layered two-tone dash, angular mirrors and a clip-on-style bar; the Standard has a 3D digital display and soft rubber grips, the SP a 4.2-inch colour TFT with Y-Connect navigation ([Inside Racing](https://insideracing.com.ph/?p=86957), [ZigWheels PH first look](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-feature-stories/all-new-yamaha-aerox-first-look)). Colours: Standard in **Race Blue, Glaze Blue (near-white) and Yamaha Black** (glossy fairings, matte cladding); SP exclusively in **Matte Blue** ([ZigWheels PH colours](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-feature-stories/here-are-the-all-new-yamaha-aeroxs-available-colors)). An "Aerox S 155" label (used in the research brief) maps to the old Mio Aerox S/S ABS (2017 to about 2024) or, for today's bike, the SP; the old body had split twin pods and body-matched six-spoke wheels ([BikeWale](https://www.bikewale.com/news/2025-yamaha-aerox-155-launched-with-minor-updates/), search summary only). **Confidence: dimensions and colour names High; body Medium; exhaust Low (no source describes it); Standard rim colour unknown.**

### Yamaha NMAX 155: crest nose and slim tail on an unchanged short-wheelbase body

The 2025 NMAX (Philippine sales from 15 March 2025) keeps the old silhouette but gets a redesigned front clip with "an upward crest in the middle" and projector LED headlights, and drops the butterfly-style tail lights and boxy rear for a slimmer design matching the front ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2025-yamaha-nmax-tech-max-specs-prices-a5361-20250315)). A first-ride review reports dual projectors, a two-level seat and footboards, and a general resemblance to the latest XMAX ([ZigWheels PH first ride](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-expert-reviews/first-ride-yamaha-nmax-tech-max)). The Tech MAX differs from the base model mainly by **gold-coloured appliques on the cast wheels and gold seat stitching** ([Carmudi](https://carmudi.com.ph/journal/yamaha-motor-ph-starts-all-new-nmax-deliveries-to-clients)). Wheels are 13 inches at both ends with discs front and rear, ABS, a telescopic fork and a unit-swing rear ([MotoDeal](https://www.motodeal.com.ph/motorcycles/yamaha/nmax-155)). The cockpit has a full digital speedometer on the base model, while the Tech MAX adds a 4.2-inch colour TFT with Garmin StreetCross navigation and Y-Connect ([Carmudi](https://carmudi.com.ph/journal/yamaha-motor-ph-starts-all-new-nmax-deliveries-to-clients)).

Do not model a "Connected" or "ABS" variant: no Philippine source lists one, and "NMAX Connected" is the Indonesian name for the previous generation. Two colour lists conflict, and the report cannot settle which is current. Launch coverage gives Standard in Black and Light Grey, Tech MAX in Black and Dark Magma; a MotoDeal page labelled 2026 gives Black and Sword Gray (Standard) and Black and Ceramic Gray (Tech MAX) ([Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/yamaha-motor-ph-turns-over-all-new-nmax-initial-units-to-owners), [MotoDeal](https://www.motodeal.com.ph/motorcycles/yamaha/nmax-155)). Tyre sizes (110/70-13, 130/70-13) come from a [ZigWheels PH FAQ](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/yamaha/nmax/faq) that may describe the previous generation, and ground clearance is 135 mm for the 2025 model against 125 mm in older listings. **Confidence: body Medium; running gear Low; colours Low; dimensions Medium.**

### Yamaha XMAX 300: X-motif sport-GT with a 2025 electric screen

The XMAX is the largest scooter in the set and the 2025 Philippine update was minor: the body, engine, dimensions and tyres are unchanged, while the update brings an electrically adjustable windscreen, a new 4.2-inch TFT plus 3.2-inch LCD cluster, improved backlighting, Garmin StreetCross navigation and a USB-C socket ([Carmudi](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/refreshed-yamaha-xmax-now-in-ph), [ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/yamaha-motor-ph-quietly-brings-in-updated-xmax)). It keeps the "X-design" front and rear fascia, and the original 2018 launch highlighted full-LED lighting with DRL front and rear, an adjustable windshield and underseat room for two full-face helmets ([Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/yamaha-ph-launches-the-xmax-300.html)). European 2025 copy describes a reshaped nose, dual LED headlight, high-mounted integrated front signals and screen travel of 95 mm, but no Philippine source confirms that this nose reached the Philippine bike ([autoevolution](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/yamaha-s-smash-hit-max-sport-scooters-get-2025-refresh-and-upgrades-245714.html)). Wheels are 15 inches front and 14 inches rear; the European Tech MAX has a 267 mm front disc with a two-piston caliper and a 245 mm rear disc ([Motofichas](https://www.motofichas.com/marcas/yamaha/xmax-300-tech-max)), while Carmudi's "single disc" wording for the Philippine bike most likely means a single front disc.

Philippine colours are **Ceramic Gray and Sword Gray** (PHP 311,000), against Bronze and Matte Gray in 2018 ([Carmudi](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/refreshed-yamaha-xmax-now-in-ph), [Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/yamaha-ph-launches-the-xmax-300.html)). Graphics are tonal and minimal. Rim colour, mirror shape, exhaust finish and shock count are unsourced. Distinguish it from the NMAX by size, the tall screen, the wheel-size step and the cluster. **Confidence: dimensions High; body Medium; PH nose detail Low; colours Medium.**

## Underbones and nakeds: the Suzuki pair is documented, the Rouser is a proxy

### Suzuki Raider R150: a faired sport underbone, now with winglets

The Philippine Raider R150 line was relaunched in November 2025 as the fifth generation (Blade Edition and Pro), and a Shadow Edition followed in October 2026 ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/suzuki-raider-r150-2026-launch-a5361-20251117), [Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/25/suzuki-adds-shadow-to-raider)). The Blade is described as "air-slicing" with plane-inspired winglets and "sharper, more dynamic lines", with full-LED head and tail lamps and a digital cluster ([Lifestyle On Wheels](https://lifestyleonwheels.com/raider-r150-blade-long-live-the-king/), [Two and Four PH](https://www.twoandfour.ph/2026/sharpening-the-king-the-suzuki-raider-r150-blade-edition/)). The Pro adds a wider Gixxer-like LED headlamp, front ABS, keyless ignition and a smartphone-linked cluster ([Carmudi](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/suzuki-raider-r150-pro-blade-edition-break-cover/), [ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/suzuki-ph-launches-raider-r150-pro-blade-edition)). The older Raider R150 Fi (2017 to 2025) has a steel twin-spar frame, a "meaner, tougher" headlight and a more aggressive muffler than earlier Raiders ([Top Gear PH 2017](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-review/review-suzuki-raider-r150-fi-a1565-20171106), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_Raider_150)). The "Raider J Crossover" in the original brief was not researched.

Running gear: 17-inch cast mags, two-piston front caliper with petal (wavy) discs front and rear, a telescopic fork and a rear mono-shock ([Premiumbikes.ph](https://premiumbikes.ph/product/suzuki-raider-r150-blade-edition/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_Raider_150)). MY2022 rim faces were black on Stellar Blue and Candy Matte Bordeaux Red bikes and red on Titan Black and Pearl Bright Ivory bikes ([CarGuide.PH](https://www.carguide.ph/2022/04/suzuki-raider-r150-fi-adds-new-colors.html)). MY2026 Blade colours are **Pearl Bright Ivory, Candy Matte Bordeaux Red, Metallic Matte Fibroin Gray and Metallic Medium Blue**; Pro comes in Bordeaux Red with Titan Black accents or Metallic Matte Platinum Silver with Tech Yellow accents; Shadow is Solid Black or Pearl Bright Ivory ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/suzuki-raider-r150-2026-launch-a5361-20251117), [Daily Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/25/suzuki-adds-shadow-to-raider)). Optional "Pintados" and "Sarimanok" decal sets exist. Silhouette cues, in priority order: the sharp faired nose and lamp, the winglets (MY2026), petal discs at both ends, 17-inch mags with contrasting rim faces, and a long low seat to a pointed tail. Tank shape, side covers, tail lamp and muffler finish are undocumented. **Confidence: colour names High; dimensions Medium (1,960 versus 1,995 mm length conflict); body Medium; muffler Low.**

### Suzuki Smash 115 and Smash Fi: a GSX-R-flavoured leisure underbone

The Smash 115 (replacing the Smash 110) has GSX-R-inspired front fairing styling, a high-wattage multi-reflector headlight, a multi-polygon-reflector tail lamp, a "sports-watch-inspired" speedometer pod with gear indicator, a large 100 mm muffler with built-in catalyzer and an 8 L underseat bin ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/news/suzuki-philippines-launches-all-new-smash-115)). The **Smash Fi** (113 cc, November 2023, MY2024) "retains the sporty and iconic look" with redesigned decals, a sportier meter and muffler, and an engine heat guard covering the leg side, so one body shell with two decal sets can serve both ([Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html)). Wheels come two ways: wire spokes with drum brakes, or alloy mags with a front disc and rear drum; both use 70/90-17 and 80/90-17 tyres ([Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html), [suzukicycles.org](https://suzukicycles.org/Smash/Smash-Fi-115.shtml)). Suspension is a telescopic fork and a rear swing arm with coil springs; the shock count is not stated, and the twin-shock assumption is the modeller's.

Smash Fi colours at launch were Candy Summer Red and Titan Black on the wire-wheel version, and Metallic Matte Stellar Blue and Titan Black on the alloy version ([Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html)). The carbureted Smash 115 had many more, including Candy Jackal Green ("a unique mix of blue and green") and Metallic Triton Blue with Cool Silver ([ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/suzuki/smash-115), [Motopinas 2019](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-offers-new-color-for-2019-smash-115.html)). Decal placement is not described anywhere. **Confidence: colour names Medium; body shape Low (no tank, side-cover, fender or tail description); Smash Fi dimensions Low (borrow the Smash 115's).**

### Kawasaki Rouser NS125 Fi: model the Bajaj Pulsar NS125 body

Kawasaki's Philippine page was unreachable, so everything rests on a "rebadged Pulsar NS125" statement from ZigWheels PH plus matching descriptions ([ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi)). The Rouser launched in 2020 at PHP 63,000 as a "sleek and edgy" bike with a semi-digital panel (analogue tacho, digital display) ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2020-kawasaki-ns125-fi-a959-20200622)). Use the Pulsar proxy dimensions (2,012 × 810 × 1,078 mm, wheelbase 1,353 mm) with caution, because the Philippine tank is 10.5 L against the Indian 12 L and the wheel colour (bronze in India) is unconfirmed. Graphics, tank-extension shape, tail lamp and muffler are unsourced. The ZigWheels PH image gallery, which captions the tank, silencer, brakes and tyres, is the best next reference ([gallery](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi/images)). **Confidence: Low overall; colour names Medium.**

## Big sportbikes and workhorses: three rich records and one photo

### Kawasaki Ninja H2: mirror-black supercharged hypersport on a green trellis

The H2 is the best documented bike in the set. Kawasaki's styling concept is "Intense Force Design"; the bodywork is very aerodynamic, with a mono-headlight tucked into the middle of the nose, a seat that tapers at the front, a tail unit that adjusts to three side-bolster positions, a first-for-Kawasaki **bright green tubular steel trellis frame** and a **single-sided swingarm** that lets the right-side silencer sit close to the frame ([Visordown](https://www.visordown.com/reviews/first-ride/first-ride-kawasaki-ninja-h2-and-h2r-review), [Cycle News](https://www.cyclenews.com/2014/11/article/first-look-2015-kawasaki-ninja-h2-street/), [Auto Trader UK](https://www.autotrader.co.uk/bikes/content/kawasaki-ninja-h2-review-super-sports-2014)). The street H2 has mirrors where the track-only H2R has winglets, and plastic panels instead of carbon ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawasaki_Ninja_H2)). The 2025 sheet lists dual 330 mm semi-floating discs with radial Brembo Stylema four-piston calipers, a 250 mm rear disc, a 43 mm inverted KYB fork and an Ohlins TTX36 rear shock with piggyback reservoir ([Kawasaki USA](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs)). Wheels are five-spoke cast aluminium; the stainless street exhaust ends in a noticeably larger right-side muffler ([Cycle News](https://www.cyclenews.com/2014/11/article/first-look-2015-kawasaki-ninja-h2-street/)). The asymmetry matters: the right rear view is dominated by the swingarm and muffler, while the left shows the chain.

The cockpit is an all-digital TFT with LED lighting throughout (the TFT, LED, Stylema and self-healing paint arrived with the 2019 update, and the bike has changed little since 2015) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawasaki_Ninja_H2)). The 2025 and 2026 US base bike carries a single colour, **Mirror Coated Spark Black**, which looks black in shade and silver in bright light ([Kawasaki USA](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs), [Cycle News](https://www.cyclenews.com/2014/11/article/first-look-2015-kawasaki-ninja-h2-street/)). The H2 Carbon uses Mirror Coated Matte Spark Black with Candy Flat Blazed Green, and the H2R is wider (33.5 versus 30.3 in) on slicks ([Kawasaki USA Carbon](https://kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-carbon-abs), [Kawasaki USA H2R](https://kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2r-abs)). Philippine availability is unclear: the only local listing found is the Carbon at about PHP 1.917 million ([Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/ninja-h2/price/makati-city/)). **Confidence: dimensions, hardware and US colours High; fairing intake shapes, fixed aero fins, tail-lamp shape, fork and caliper colours unsourced.**

### Honda CBR650R: a baby-Fireblade fairing, restyled again in 2024

The 2019 generation introduced "Neo Sports Cafe" styling with a Fireblade-style fairing, dual front lamps, ram-air intakes under the lamp and a lighter frame; 2021 changed the tail section and plate holder; the 2024 RH17 restyle reworked the upper and lower fairings, dual LED headlights ("a penetrating stare"), the tail unit and tail light, and the seat, and added a 5-inch TFT and optional E-Clutch ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_650_standard_and_sport_motorcycles), [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r), [Ultimate Motorcycling](https://ultimatemotorcycling.com/2023/11/09/2024-honda-cbr650r-and-cb650r-first-look-with-e-clutch/)). The fairing is tightly wrapped, the seat unit minimal, the rear mudguard steel with a number-plate mount, and twin air ducts flank the tank; clip-ons sit under the top yoke over rear-set pegs ([Lloyd Motor Group](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/NewCars/Honda-Motorcycles/CBR/CBR650R/2238), [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)). Reviewers say the nose looks like a baby Fireblade and that the optional E-Clutch unit sprouts visibly from the engine's side ([Autotrader UK](https://www.autotrader.co.uk/bikes/content/honda-cbr650r-eclutch_review_2024)).

Hardware: 17-inch hollow-section six-spoke cast wheels (2024 spec; 2019 was reported as Y-spoke), 3.5 and 5.5 in rim widths, dual 310 mm floating discs with radial four-piston calipers, a 240 mm rear disc, a 41 mm Showa SFF-BP inverted fork, a preload-adjustable monoshock, and four downpipes sweeping round the front of the engine into a collector and an angled muffler (Philippine listing: "4-into-1") ([Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r-eclutch), [Lloyd Motor Group](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/NewCars/Honda-Motorcycles/CBR/CBR650R/2238), [Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/e-clutch)). The Philippine launch colours (February 2022) were **Grand Prix Red and Matte Gunpowder Black Metallic**; Europe's 2024 red is the Grand Prix Red Tricolour (red, white and blue), and the 2026 European black adds a yellow accent along the side fairing with no mechanical change ([Top Gear PH 2022](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2022-honda-cbr650r-ph-launch-a4354-20220216), [Visordown](https://www.visordown.com/news/honda-cb650r-and-cbr650r-receives-colour-overhaul-2026)). The decisive open question is whether the Philippine bike on sale is the 2024 restyle with TFT or the 2021-body bike with an LCD; the Philippine 2022 launch article describes the LCD version, and no Philippine source confirms the 2024 body. **Confidence: dimensions High; body Medium; Philippine specification Low; fork and rim colour unsourced.**

### Honda TMX Supremo: a plain, tall workhorse built for sidecars

The TMX Supremo (a rebadged Honda GL150 per Wikipedia, 150 cc air-cooled SOHC, 5-speed) is a utilitarian backbone-frame standard with a flat dual seat, lockable polypropylene side covers, a chrome silencer heat shield, a tubular grab rail with three rear luggage points, a halogen headlight, a "simple bulb" tail light, a two-pod console above the headlight (tacho left, odometer right, fuel gauge middle) and factory three-point sidecar mounts ([Zigwheels PH features](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/honda-tmx-supremo-features-explained), [Gadgets Magazine](https://gadgetsmagazine.com.ph/?p=52563), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX), [Motortrade](https://motortrade.com.ph/motorcycles/honda-tmx-supremo-150-tmxs2j-motortrade-motrcycle/)). The third generation (2018, current) looks "a bit subdued" next to the previous one but carries "snazzy graphics"; the 2015 second generation introduced a new tank with an "elegant sticker", dual flat seats and the PP side covers ([Zigwheels PH features](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/honda-tmx-supremo-features-explained), [Gadgets Magazine](https://gadgetsmagazine.com.ph/?p=52563)). Reviewers call it plain and dated.

Running gear: 18-inch wire-spoke wheels with tubes, mechanical drum brakes at both ends, a telescopic fork and twin rear shocks ([Wheeltek](https://wheeltek.com.ph/vehicles/tmx-supremo/), [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications)). Colours are Candy (Ruby) Red and Fiery Black at PHP 78,900 ([Zigwheels PH features](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/honda-tmx-supremo-features-explained), [Wheeltek](https://wheeltek.com.ph/vehicles/tmx-supremo/)). Ground clearance spans 156-165 mm and width 766-778 mm across dealers, so use roughly 163 mm and 770 mm; a 14.3 L tank in one dealer listing is an outlier against 10.3 L elsewhere. The headlight outline, tail-lamp lens, tank-graphic placement, rim and hub colour, and engine fin pattern are undocumented. The repo's data file is named `tmx155` but describes the Supremo 150; the older TMX 155 (1981-2014) was a 4-speed OHV bike, so keep the Supremo references ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX)). **Confidence: running gear and features Medium; body outline Low; graphics Low.**

### Rusi Macho 125: a chrome-trimmed CG clone with a distinctive headlight shroud

Kamote's blog calls the Macho 125 "the Chinese copy of the TMX 125 but more affordable", and its backbone frame, 125 cc air-cooled SOHC carbureted single, 5-speed gearbox, electric and kick start, analogue display and halogen lighting are consistent across listings ([Kamote blog](https://kamote.ph/blog/top-7-rusi-motorcycles), [Kamote](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125), [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125)). ## Source conflicts concentrate on which generation, not on geometry

Most disagreements are about identity rather than size. The Click's "125i" label covers two bodies, and aggregators attach the 2021 colour set and 2018 dimensions to a 2026 date, so any Click number should be checked against the 2026 Top Gear PH and Motodeal figures ([Motodeal](https://motodeal.com.ph/motorcycles/honda/click-125/standard)). Motodeal also lists a Street trim and Smart Edition colours that Top Gear PH's two-variant coverage omits. The NMAX has two incompatible Philippine colour lists, a 125 versus 135 mm ground-clearance conflict, tyre sizes of uncertain generation, and two descriptions of the Tech MAX cluster ("two small digital displays" versus "4.2-inch colour TFT") that probably describe the same hardware ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2025-yamaha-nmax-tech-max-specs-prices-a5361-20250315), [Carmudi](https://carmudi.com.ph/journal/yamaha-motor-ph-starts-all-new-nmax-deliveries-to-clients)). The Aerox rear suspension is described both as "two shock absorbers" and as a "sub-tank", so twin shocks with one remote reservoir is an inference. The CBR650R's Philippine specification (2022 LCD or 2024 TFT), its wheel pattern (Y-spoke in 2019 versus six hollow spokes in 2024) and the Fireblade it imitates (2017 CBR1000RR versus CBR1000RR-R) are all unsettled. The Ninja H2 has no Philippine base-trim confirmation.

Numeric conflicts are smaller but real. The Raider's MY2026 length is 1,995 mm in Top Gear PH against 1,960 mm in every earlier listing (a nose extension or a typo), its Blade and Pro weights are 112 and 115 kg in the fetched text but "identical" in a search summary of the same article, and a 3.7, 4.2 and 4.3 L tank spread, a 750, 755 and 765 mm seat spread and a 130 versus 145 mm clearance conflict clutter the Smash record ([Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/suzuki-raider-r150-2026-launch-a5361-20251117), [Carmudi Smash Fi](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/suzuki/smash-fi)). ZigWheels PH says the Smash "lacks alloy wheels", contradicting the existence of a mags version. The TMX has clearance and width spreads across four dealers, a 14.3 L tank outlier and a Carmudi page that lists ABS and a backrest that other sources deny. The Rouser has a 10.5 versus 10.2 L tank and a search snippet naming "Pearl Metallic Silver and Sparkling Black" that contradicts the Black and Yellow listings.

Provenance risks are worth stating plainly. The Rouser rests on a single ZigWheels "rebadge" statement plus matching descriptions, with no direct Kawasaki confirmation. The Macho's two spec sources trace to one lineage, its only picture is one side-on photo, and Kamote's colour field is unreliable (it lists one colour for the 150 and 175 while the photos show others). Several NMAX, Smash, Raider and Click claims were read only from search-result summaries of pages that returned 403. And the research could not view any manufacturer image, so lamp geometry, vent shapes, grab-rail outlines and tail sections are the largest unmeasured quantities in the entire set.

## Conclusion

The record turns out to be an inverted pyramid: it is strong for the iconic, well-marketed bike (the H2) and for the 2025 Yamaha launches, and weakest for the cheap workhorses and the proxy-based Rouser. That inversion has a practical remedy, because the weak bikes have strong lineages that fill the gaps: the Macho follows the Honda CG125 and TMX 125 Alpha template, the TMX Supremo is a GL150, the Rouser is a Pulsar NS125, and the Aerox and 2025 NMAX borrow XMAX lighting and fascia language, so family resemblance can bridge geometry that no text describes. The two workhorses, the TMX Supremo and the Macho, share a layout, and the documented differences are wheel size (18 versus 17 inches), the Macho's chrome rack, front fender and headlight shroud, and the TMX's chrome pipe shield.

The more important insight is that most of the risk sits in naming, not measurement. Six of the eleven bikes (Click, Smash, Aerox, NMAX, CBR650R, Raider) changed look since late 2022, and the labels "Click 125i" and "Raider R150 Fi" name the previous generation of two of them, so the first modelling decision for each is the model year. Settle that, confirm the top four photo checks, and the remaining work is detail refinement and colour sampling.
