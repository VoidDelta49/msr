CPM: Motor Edition

Free-roam island riding

CHOOSE YOUR BIKE

Share the room code with friends (up to 8 per room). Ghost mode: bikes ride through each other. Tap a rider's name tag to challenge them: a plain sprint, a parcel race (crash or hit a wall and the parcel is lost) or a food race (bumps and hard braking spoil it, which adds time).

Server (optional)

Ride an island town: coastal highway, beaches, a volcano, rice terraces, a river with bridges, a switchback mountain pass and an airstrip for top-speed runs. Quests: beacons are scattered over the island at random (blue P = parking challenge in the lot, tan B = parcel delivery, pink F = food delivery) and pinned on the minimap. Ride in and hold still to take one. A red outline means that job needs a specific bike: you are switched to it on the spot and get less time. A finished job's beacon moves elsewhere. Fuel up at the station next to the lot (red dot on the map).
