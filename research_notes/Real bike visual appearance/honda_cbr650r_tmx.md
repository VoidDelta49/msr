# Real-world visual appearance: Honda CBR650R and Honda TMX Supremo (Philippine market, current to 2026)

Research date: 2026-11-06. Method note: honda official PH pages (hondaphil.com / hondaph.com) returned 404/500 or could not be retrieved, so Philippine specs come from aggregators and dealers (Zigwheels PH, Carmudi PH, Wheeltek, Motortrade, Bikes4sale) cross-checked against UK/Europe Honda-derived coverage (Total Motorcycle, Lloyd Motor Group Honda dealer page, Autotrader UK, Visordown, Ultimate Motorcycling). No photos could be inspected by this research tool; all appearance claims are from text. Hex values below are my own approximations (labelled as such under Inferences), not sourced.

Game-relevant repo note: the repo already has `data/bikes/cbr650r.json` and `data/bikes/tmx155.json`. This research did not read them.

## 1. Overall silhouette and body design language

### Takeaway
CBR650R: a fully faired middleweight sportbike with a steel diamond frame, "Neo Sports Cafe" / baby-Fireblade styling (dual slanted LED headlights, tight fairing, truncated tail, minimal seat unit); the 2024 update (restyled fairing, headlights, seat/tail, tail light, 5-inch TFT) is the current look. TMX Supremo: a plain, utilitarian "standard"/backbone-frame commuter-workhorse with a flat dual seat, polypropylene side covers with lock, chrome silencer heat shield, tubular grab rail, simple halogen headlight and bulb tail light, built for sidecar/tricycle duty; reviewers call the styling plain and "a bit subdued" or dated.

### Cited Findings
CBR650R
- Fully faired version; the CB650R is "essentially" the CBR650R with the fairing removed and cafe-racer styling — [Visordown 2024 launch article](https://visordown.com/news/new-bikes/2024-honda-cbr650r-debuts-all-new-tech)
- 2019 (RH01 generation) introduced "Neo Sports Cafe" styling aligned with CB125R/CB300R/CB1000R; the sport model got a Fireblade-style fairing and dual front lamps, a lighter rigid frame, and the clip-on bar moved lower under the upper triple clamp — [Wikipedia, Honda 650 standard and sport motorcycles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_650_standard_and_sport_motorcycles)
- 2019 model had dual ram-air intakes under the front lamp and a larger exhaust pipe and muffler; full LED lighting and an LCD dash with gear indicator and shift light — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_650_standard_and_sport_motorcycles)
- 2024 (RH17): bodywork described as aggressive, race-inspired; redesigned upper and lower fairings that "blend muscularity with slim lines and angles"; redesigned dual LED headlights that "emit a penetrating stare"; redesigned tail unit with LED tail light; more compact seat unit that "neatly truncates the rear"; revised rider/pillion seat shaping — [Total Motorcycle 2024 CBR650R Guide](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)
- 2024 styling: new front full fairing takes inspiration from the latest-generation CBR1000RR; reworked dual LED headlights; tail section revised — [Ultimate Motorcycling first look](https://ultimatemotorcycling.com/2023/11/09/2024-honda-cbr650r-and-cb650r-first-look-with-e-clutch/); [Bikes Republic](https://bikesrepublic.com/featured/2024-honda-cbr650r-updated-with-new-design-tech) (search snippet only). Cycle World's first-look page confirms only that "new styling" is among the 2024 changes ([Cycle World](https://cycleworld.com/bikes/honda-cbr650r-and-cb650r-first-look)); a search-result summary also mentioned a "slanted LED headlight, new radiator shrouds and a sharper rear fairing" attributed to Honda copy, but I could not identify the page it came from, so treat that wording as unverified.
- Dealer copy (UK Honda dealer, 2024 model): bike is styled for high-speed aerodynamics with a tightly wrapped fairing; seat unit is minimal, side panels slimmer and reshaped; rear number-plate mount restyled; dual LED headlights with new reflectors, "crisp white" beam — [Lloyd Motor Group Honda CBR650R page](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/NewCars/Honda-Motorcycles/CBR/CBR650R/2238)
- Side panels and a steel rear mudguard / number-plate mount add to the minimalist look; clip-on bars sit beneath the top yoke with rear-set footpegs — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)
- Reviewer: fairing "more aggressive than on the 2019 original", resembles a "baby 'blade" at a glance; seat reprofiled; tail light "sculpted"; the E-Clutch mechanism "sprouts from the side of the engine" and the reviewer finds it slightly unsightly — [Autotrader UK CBR650R E-Clutch review 2024](https://www.autotrader.co.uk/bikes/content/honda-cbr650r-eclutch_review_2024)
- Philippine listing: sport category, side wings, "dual-stepped seat", steel diamond frame, 4-into-1 exhaust, LED headlamp and LED tail lamp, side reflectors — [Carmudi PH CBR650R E-Clutch](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/e-clutch); [Zigwheels PH CBR650R specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/specifications)
- Engine is a visual focus: 649 cc liquid-cooled DOHC 16-valve inline-four, cylinders canted forward 30 degrees, twin air ducts either side of the fuel tank feeding the airbox — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)
- Optional accessory packs (Europe): Racing pack = carbon-look tank pad with CBR logo, transparent tank grippers, colour-matched rear seat cover; wheel stripe kit available — [Visordown](https://visordown.com/news/new-bikes/2024-honda-cbr650r-debuts-all-new-tech)

TMX Supremo
- Utilitarian sidecar/tricycle-oriented commuter; 150 cc air-cooled OHC single, 5-speed, kick and electric start; "dual straight" seat; chrome garnish — [Zigwheels PH TMX Supremo specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications)
- Frame type listed as "Backbone" by a dealer — [Motortrade TMX Supremo](https://motortrade.com.ph/motorcycles/honda-tmx-supremo-150-tmxs2j-motortrade-motrcycle/)
- Zigwheels feature article (Oct 31, 2018, third-gen): chrome heat shield covers the silencer; three luggage attachment points on the rear panel plus a tubular grab rail that can carry some luggage; halogen headlight; "tail light is a simple bulb affair"; two-pod console above the headlight; raised handlebar carries headlight controls and pass-light switch; "snazzy graphics" but overall look "a bit subdued" compared with the previous generation — [Zigwheels PH, Honda TMX Supremo: Features explained](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/honda-tmx-supremo-features-explained)
- Second generation (Gadgets Magazine, 7 Aug 2015): new fuel tank design with an "elegant sticker", new dual flat seats, improved/stronger polypropylene (PP) side cover with security lock, factory-installed three-point sidecar attachment — [Gadgets Magazine PH](https://gadgetsmagazine.com.ph/?p=52563); same points summarised in [Wikipedia, Honda TMX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX)
- Large airbox is hidden under stock side covers and plastic (builder's account of a TMX 150 Supremo) — [Bikebound](https://www.bikebound.com/2018/09/23/honda-tmx-150-tracker/)
- Reviewers/owners: looks "plain and dated"; spoke wheels and side covers help with rough Metro Manila roads — [Zigwheels PH user reviews](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/user-reviews)
- Wikipedia: the TMX Supremo (2012-present) is a rebadged Honda GL150; successor to the TMX 155 (1981-2014, OHV 155 cc, 4-speed, drum brakes, telescopic fork, twin rear shocks) — [Wikipedia, Honda TMX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX)

### Inferences
- CBR650R silhouette for a model: a tight fairing nose with two slanted LED lamps (2019 spec had ram-air intakes between/below them), a waisted tank with twin air ducts either side, and a short, upswept tail with a steel number-plate hanger (no long tail tidy). It reads as a scaled Fireblade (2017 CBR1000RR-era per search summary; Total Motorcycle copy says CBR1000RR-R, so the exact Fireblade generation is uncertain).
- TMX Supremo silhouette for a model: tall, narrow, upright standard bike with a long flat seat, large 18-inch spoked wheels, tall ground clearance, fully exposed engine, chrome silencer shield, simple round-ish halogen headlight, tubular rear grab rail/rack, and plain side covers hiding the airbox.
- "Backbone" frame (Motortrade) is consistent with Honda's classic underbone/standard single-down-tube design, but see Gaps: no photo confirmation.

### Gaps
- Official Honda PH pages could not be fetched (404/500), so no first-party design copy for either bike.
- No retrieved text describes TMX headlight shape (round vs. trapezoid), tank graphics placement, fender material/colour, or exact rack/carrier style; Zigwheels lists a "tubular grab rail" and three luggage attachment points, not a flat cargo rack.
- Which Fireblade the 2024 CBR650R mimics is inconsistent across sources (2017 CBR1000RR vs. CBR1000RR-R).

## 2. Key dimensions

### Takeaway
CBR650R (PH listing): about 2120 x 750 x 1150 mm, wheelbase 1450, seat 810, ground clearance 130, 208 kg kerb, 120/70ZR17 front and 180/55ZR17 rear, 15.4 L tank. TMX Supremo: about 2041 x 771 x 1071 mm, wheelbase 1306, seat 782, ground clearance 156-165 (sources differ), about 121 kg dry, 80/100-18 front and 90/90-18 rear on spoked wheels, 10.3 L tank.

### Cited Findings
CBR650R
- PH listing: L 2120, W 750, H 1150, wheelbase 1450, seat 810, ground clearance 130, kerb 208 kg, fuel tank 15.4 L; tyres 120/70 ZR17 and 180/55 ZR17 radial tubeless — [Zigwheels PH specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/specifications); same figures in [Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/e-clutch)

- Rim widths 3.5 in front, 5.5 in rear — [Lloyd Motor Group](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/NewCars/Honda-Motorcycles/CBR/CBR650R/2238)
- Frame: steel diamond — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r); [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/specifications)
- Battery 12 V / 8.6 Ah, oil 2.7 L (not visual; context) — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)

TMX Supremo
- Zigwheels PH / Carmudi PH: L 2041, W 771, H 1071, wheelbase 1306, seat 782, ground clearance 165, dry 121 kg; tyres 80/100-18 front, 90/90-18 rear; fuel 10.3 L, oil 1.2 L — [Zigwheels PH specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications); [Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications)
- Wheeltek (dealer): L 2038, W 766, H 1065, seat 782, ground clearance 163, curb 127 kg, tyres 80/100-18 M/C 47P front and 90/90-18 M/C 51P rear (with tube), fuel 10.3 L — [Wheeltek](https://wheeltek.com.ph/vehicles/tmx-supremo/)
- Motortrade (dealer): L 2037, W 778, H 1068, wheelbase 1306, seat 771, ground clearance 163, dry 120 kg; fuel capacity printed as 14.3 L with 2.0 L reserve (conflicts with 10.3 L elsewhere) — [Motortrade](https://motortrade.com.ph/motorcycles/honda-tmx-supremo-150-tmxs2j-motortrade-motrcycle/)
- Bikes4sale (3rd generation): 2041 x 771 x 1071, seat 782, wheelbase 1306, ground clearance 156, 121 kg, fuel 10.3 L — [Bikes4sale](https://bikes4sale.com/details/honda/tmx-supremo-3rd-generation)

### Inferences
- Reasonable model numbers: CBR650R 2120/750/1150 mm, WB 1450, seat 810, 208-209 kg; TMX Supremo 2040/770/1070 mm, WB 1306, seat 782, 121 kg dry (about 127 kg curb per Wheeltek).
- The TMX ground clearance spread (156 / 163 / 165 mm) and width spread (766-778 mm) are within normal spec-sheet noise; pick about 163 mm and about 770 mm. 10.3 L is the majority fuel-tank figure; 14.3 L (Motortrade) looks like a typo.

### Gaps
- No official Honda PH spec sheet could be retrieved for either bike.
- Handlebar width, mirror span, footpeg height, rear-carrier dimensions and exact fender dimensions were not found.
- Carmudi's TMX page also lists ABS, height-adjustable seat, side wings and backrest, which contradict Zigwheels (which says none of those); treated as aggregator error, not used.

## 3. Wheels, tyres, brakes, suspension, exhaust, engine appearance

### Takeaway
CBR650R: 17-inch hollow-section 6-spoke cast aluminium wheels (2024 Europe spec; 2019 reported as Y-spoke), dual 310 mm floating front discs with radial 4-piston calipers, 240 mm rear disc, 41 mm Showa SFF-BP USD fork (finish/colour not sourced), preload-adjustable monoshock, four-into-one exhaust sweeping under/around the engine. TMX Supremo: 18-inch wire-spoke wheels (tube tyres), drum brakes front and rear, telescopic fork, twin rear shocks, single chrome-shielded silencer, exposed air-cooled finned single.

### Cited Findings
CBR650R
- Wheels: hollow-section 6-spoke cast aluminium, 17 in — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r-eclutch); [Visordown](https://visordown.com/news/new-bikes/2024-honda-cbr650r-debuts-all-new-tech)
- Brakes: dual 310 mm floating discs with four-piston radial-mount calipers (Nissin per PH launch coverage), single 240 mm rear disc with single-piston caliper, 2-channel ABS — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r-eclutch); [Top Gear PH 2022 launch](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2022-honda-cbr650r-ph-launch-a4354-20220216)
- Suspension: 41 mm Showa SFF-BP USD fork; monoshock with 10-stage preload adjustment, 43.5 mm stroke — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r-eclutch)
- 2019 generation: "lighter cast Y-spoke aluminum wheels", radial-mount front calipers, inverted Showa separate-function fork — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_650_standard_and_sport_motorcycles) (contradicts the "hollow 6-spoke" 2024 description; likely a wheel redesign or loose wording)
- Exhaust: four downpipes sweep together around the front of the engine into a collector box and muffler; muffler angled to direct sound toward the rider; Euro 5 via collector box and muffler changes — [Lloyd Motor Group](https://www.lloydmotorgroup.com/NewCars/Honda-Motorcycles/CBR/CBR650R/2238); PH listing says "4-into-1 exhaust" — [Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/e-clutch)
- Crankcase cover colour: Wikipedia notes bronze crankcase on 2017 CBR650F and "still bronze" in the 2021 update — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_650_standard_and_sport_motorcycles)
- Engine detail: liquid-cooled DOHC inline-four, bore x stroke 67.0 x 46.0 mm — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)

TMX Supremo
- 18-inch spoked wheels; Honda says spoked wheels suit local conditions and heavy daily loads — [Zigwheels PH features article](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/honda-tmx-supremo-features-explained)
- Wire-spoke wheels, 80/100-18 front and 90/90-18 rear (with tube) — [Wheeltek](https://wheeltek.com.ph/vehicles/tmx-supremo/); [Motortrade](https://motortrade.com.ph/motorcycles/honda-tmx-supremo-150-tmxs2j-motortrade-motrcycle/)
- Brakes: mechanical leading-trailing drum front and rear — [Wheeltek](https://wheeltek.com.ph/vehicles/tmx-supremo/); [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications)
- Suspension: telescopic front fork, twin rear shocks — [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications)
- Exhaust: single silencer under a chrome heat shield — [Zigwheels PH features](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/honda-tmx-supremo-features-explained); [Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications) ("single exhaust")
- Engine: 149.2 cc, 4-stroke, air-cooled, OHC, carburetted; 5-speed, chain drive — [Motortrade](https://motortrade.com.ph/motorcycles/honda-tmx-supremo-150-tmxs2j-motortrade-motrcycle/); [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications)
- Predecessor TMX 155 had an OHV engine (the Supremo is SOHC) — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX)

### Inferences
- For a model: CBR650R wheels should be 17-inch with six hollow, tapered spokes (2024 spec), rim widths 3.5 / 5.5 in, fat 180/55 rear; two big floating discs with radially mounted 4-piston calipers up front. Fork finish (black vs gold) and rim colour are not stated in any retrieved text.
- TMX wheels: thin wire spokes, 18-inch rims with relatively narrow tyres (80/100 and 90/90), small drum hubs both ends. A 5-speed, finned single-cylinder barrel, probably with a slight forward tilt, left exposed under the tank.
- The 2019 "Y-spoke" statement means that if the game builds a 2019-2021 look, wheels would be Y-spoke instead of the 2024 six-spoke; unverified.

### Gaps
- Fork-leg colour, rear-shock spring colour, swingarm design (steel vs aluminium; boxed vs. tubular), rim colour, and exhaust finish (stainless vs black; silencer end-cap shape) for the CBR650R were not found in text sources.
- TMX rim colour (silver vs black), hub colour, silencer exact shape (length/diameter), and whether a rear carrier/rack ships standard: not found (only a grab rail and luggage hooks are mentioned).
- Cooling-fin count/shape for the TMX engine not documented in retrieved text.

## 4. Instrument cluster, handlebar, mirrors, footpegs, controls

### Takeaway
CBR650R: 5-inch full-colour TFT (from 2024) with a backlit 4-way left-hand toggle, clip-on bars below the top yoke, rear-set pegs; the pre-2024 bike (still the one covered by the 2022 PH launch) had a monochrome LCD. TMX Supremo: raised, wide standard handlebar, two-pod analogue console above the headlight (tachometer left, odometer/speedo right, fuel gauge centre), telltales beneath, key shutter lock, pass switch.

### Cited Findings
CBR650R
- 2024: 5-inch full-colour TFT with optical bonding, Bar / Circle / Simple display patterns, Honda RoadSync, backlit simplified 4-way left-hand toggle switch — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r); [Visordown](https://visordown.com/news/new-bikes/2024-honda-cbr650r-debuts-all-new-tech)
- Clip-on handlebars under the top yoke, rear-set footpegs — [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)
- 2022 PH launch: LCD instrument cluster with digital speedometer, gear position, shift-up indicator, fuel consumption and water temperature — [Top Gear PH 2022](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2022-honda-cbr650r-ph-launch-a4354-20220216)
- PH listing: digital display with trip meter, oil change indicator, engine check warning; riding modes (Road / Sport) and launch control listed — [Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/e-clutch)
- Optional E-Clutch unit visible on the engine's side — [Autotrader UK](https://www.autotrader.co.uk/bikes/content/honda-cbr650r-eclutch_review_2024)

TMX Supremo
- Two-pod console above the headlight: tachometer left, odometer right, fuel gauge middle, telltales under the pods — [Zigwheels PH features](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/honda-tmx-supremo-features-explained)
- Analogue speedometer/odometer/fuel gauge, tachometer, tripmeter, no side-stand indicator; pass switch, shutter lock, chrome garnish, adjustable halogen headlamp — [Zigwheels PH specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/tmx-supremo/specifications)
- Wikipedia: illuminated meter panel with tachometer and gear shift indicator, key shutter, integrated handlebar lock, choke control on the handlebar, electric starter (plus kick) — [Wikipedia, Honda TMX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX)
- Raised handlebar carries headlight controls and pass-light switch — [Zigwheels PH features](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/honda-tmx-supremo-features-explained)

### Inferences
- A game cluster for the CBR650R should be a landscape 5-inch colour screen with a large bar tachometer along the top and digital speed in the centre; the current PH bike is probably this TFT version (see section 7 gap), but if the game is set around the earlier PH 2022 launch, it would be a smaller LCD.
- TMX: simple twin analogue pods (tach and speedometer) under a short visor-less cluster; key switch and shutter near the top of the headlight/steering stem region (exact position not documented).

### Gaps
- Mirror shape, size and mounting position for both bikes were not documented in any retrieved text.
- Footpeg/rubber design and heel-guard shape not found.
- Whether the PH-market CBR650R currently sold has the TFT (2024+) or the older LCD is not conclusively confirmed (see section 7).
- Whether PH TMX has a speedometer-in-pod layout that includes a gear indicator (Wikipedia says gear shift indicator; Zigwheels' description does not mention one) is unresolved.

## 5. Official colour schemes and graphics

### Takeaway
CBR650R PH: two colours, Grand Prix Red and Matte Gunpowder Black Metallic (2022 PH launch); aggregators still list just "Red" and "Black" for the current CBR650R. Europe 2026 changes the black to a Matt Gunpowder Black Metallic with a bright yellow accent along the side fairing; red remains "Grand Prix Red Tricolour". TMX Supremo PH: two colours, Black (Fiery Black) and Red (Candy Red / Candy Ruby Red), with a stickered fuel tank and "snazzy" graphics.

### Cited Findings
CBR650R
- PH launch (Feb 2022): Grand Prix Red and Matte Gunpowder Black Metallic — [Top Gear PH 2022](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2022-honda-cbr650r-ph-launch-a4354-20220216)
- Current PH aggregators: 2 colours, Red and Black — [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/faq/what-are-the-colors-options-available-in-honda-cbr650r); [Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/honda/cbr650r/e-clutch)

### Inferences (hex values are my own visual approximations, NOT from any source)
- CBR650R Grand Prix Red Tricolour: red body approx #C8102E to #D3202A with white and dark blue (navy approx #1C2A5A) stripe/tricolour accents (the tricolour typically sits on the fairing sides, tank and seat cowl); black fork/engine/wheels likely.

## 7. Model-year differences that change the look

### Takeaway
CBR650R: 2019 (RH01, "Neo Sports Cafe" restyle of CBR650F), 2021 (RH03: SFF-BP fork, new tail section, licence-plate holder, Euro 5), 2024 (RH17: restyled fairing/headlights/seat/tail/tail light, 5-inch TFT, optional E-Clutch), 2026 (Europe only: colours; one new black with yellow accent). TMX: 155 (1981-2014, OHV) was replaced in 2012 by the Supremo 150 (GL150-based); 2nd generation 2015 (new tank, sticker, dual flat seat, PP lockable side cover, sidecar mounts); 3rd generation 2018 (current; more subdued look, snazzy graphics, spoked wheels, chrome silencer shield).

### Cited Findings
CBR650R
- 2019 RH01: Neo Sports Cafe styling, Fireblade-style fairing, dual front lamps, lighter frame, USD Showa fork, Y-spoke wheels, ram-air intakes, larger exhaust, LED lighting, LCD dash — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_650_standard_and_sport_motorcycles)
- 2021 RH03: revised cams/mapping for Euro 5, LCD dash angle/illumination changed, Showa SFF-BP fork, new tail section, new licence-plate holder, crankcase cover (still bronze), revised geometry and ergonomics — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_650_standard_and_sport_motorcycles)
- PH launch of the CBR650R (Feb 2022): LED lighting, LCD cluster, 41 mm USD SFF-BP fork, dual 310 mm discs with Nissin radial calipers, 240 mm rear disc — [Top Gear PH 2022](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2022-honda-cbr650r-ph-launch-a4354-20220216)
- 2024 RH17: restyled front fairing (CBR1000RR-inspired), reworked dual LED headlights, revised tail section and tail light, reprofiled seat, new 5-inch TFT, new left-bar toggle, optional E-Clutch (about +2 kg) — [Ultimate Motorcycling](https://ultimatemotorcycling.com/2023/11/09/2024-honda-cbr650r-and-cb650r-first-look-with-e-clutch/); [Visordown](https://visordown.com/news/new-bikes/2024-honda-cbr650r-debuts-all-new-tech); [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/motorcycles/2024/2024-honda-cbr650r)
