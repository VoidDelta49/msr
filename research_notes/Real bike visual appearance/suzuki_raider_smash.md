# Real-world visual appearance: Suzuki Raider R150 Fi and Suzuki Smash 115 / Smash 115 Fi (Philippine market)

Scope: appearance and dimensions for a faithful 3D game model. Research date: 2026-11-06. Model year (MY) is noted per claim. Suzuki Philippines' own site (mc.suzuki.com.ph / suzuki.com.ph) returned HTTP 403 or redirected for every direct fetch, so no official spec sheet or product page was read directly. Where an "official" statement appears below it is relayed through a search-result summary and flagged as such. Photos were not viewable, so shape descriptions are limited to what text sources say.

Naming note: the current Philippine Raider R150 line (launched November 2025, branded MY2026) is called the "5th generation" by Suzuki PH and comes as Blade Edition, Pro, and (from October 2026) Shadow Edition. The older Raider R150 Fi (2017 to 2025) is the prior look. The "Raider J Crossover" name in the brief was not researched and no source on it was found (see Gaps).

## 1. Overall silhouette and body design language (frame, tank/fairing, lights, side covers, seat, tail, fenders, legshield, proportions)

### Takeaway
Raider R150: a sport underbone (tubular backbone/diamond frame, 17" front and rear, fully faired front end with a sharp headlight, rear mono-shock, disc brakes at both ends). The MY2026 5th-gen adds sharper "air-slicing" body panels with small aircraft-style winglets on the front fairing, full-LED head and tail lamps, and a full-digital cluster. The Pro variant is differentiated by a wider, Gixxer-style LED headlamp. Smash 115 / Smash Fi: a small leisure/commuter underbone, GSX-R-inspired front fairing with a wide multi-reflector headlight, a sports-watch-style speedo pod, a large 100 mm muffler, 17" wheels (spoked with drums, or cast "mags" with front disc) and an 8 L underseat bin.

### Cited Findings

Both are underbones with 17" wheels, disc brakes front and rear, a telescopic front fork and swingarm rear. — [Top Gear PH, Nov 2025](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/suzuki-raider-r150-2026-launch-a5361-20251117)

Digital cluster, one-action key shutter and Easy Start. — [Two and Four PH, 2026](https://www.twoandfour.ph/2026/sharpening-the-king-the-suzuki-raider-r150-blade-edition/)
- Blade Edition keeps the familiar Raider headlamp design with new aerodynamic body panels (per Manila Bulletin, relayed via search summary; the page itself returned 403). — [Manila Bulletin, 12 Nov 2025](https://mb.com.ph/2025/11/12/suzuki-unveils-the-updated-the-raider-r150)

**Raider R150 Fi (previous look, MY2017 to MY2025)**
- Second-generation Raider (2016 onward, Indonesia's Satria F150 sibling): digital speedometer, LED headlight, fuel injection, liquid cooling. Frame is a steel twin-spar diamond (backbone-style underbone frame). Full-length front fork, rear mono-shock, alloy cast wheels, large front disc and a rear disc ("rare on underbones"). — [Wikipedia: Suzuki Raider 150](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_Raider_150)
- Top Gear PH's Nov 2017 test of the Fi: chassis "looks much like earlier Raider models"; new headlight gives a "meaner, tougher front end"; muffler looks "much more aggressive than before"; MotoGP-style blue paint with flashy decals; wide, long, well-cushioned seat; handlebar sits very low; discreet shutter on the keyhole; small glovebox. — [Top Gear PH review, 6 Nov 2017](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-review/review-suzuki-raider-r150-fi-a1565-20171106)
- Top Gear PH buyer's guide (MY2019): Fi has LED headlight and a digital LCD cluster; Fi and Carb versions are sold side by side. — [Top Gear PH buyer's guide](https://www.topgear.com.ph/index.php/buyers-guide/motorcycles/suzuki/raider-r150)

**Smash 115 (carbureted, current generation)**
- Top Gear PH's launch coverage of the "all-new" Smash 115 (replaced the Smash 110; article undated in the fetched text): front fairing styling inspired by Suzuki's GSX-R sportbikes; high-wattage multi-reflector headlight; multi-polygon-reflector rear combination lamp; instrument pod with a "sports-watch-inspired" speedometer and gear-position indicator; large-diameter 100 mm muffler with built-in oxidation catalyzer; 8 L underseat compartment; magnetic key shutter. — [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/news/suzuki-philippines-launches-all-new-smash-115)
- Zigwheels PH describes the Smash 115 as "aerodynamic, pouncing forward, sharp design" with a racing-inspired front fascia, a wide bold headlight, graphics on the front and sides, and calls the overall look fairly plain. Page notes the Smash 115 is discontinued in PH (the Fi replaced it). — [Zigwheels PH Smash 115](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/suzuki/smash-115)
- Chassis is a diamond-type frame with 26 degree caster, 64 mm trail, 1,240 mm wheelbase; PAIR system and 100 mm muffler with oxidation catalyzer (MY2019 article). — [Motopinas, Oct 2019](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-offers-new-color-for-2019-smash-115.html)

**Smash Fi (MY2024, launched Nov 2023)**
- Suzuki PH introduced the Smash Fi (113 cc, fuel injected) in Nov 2023. It "retains the sporty and iconic look of the previous Smash 115" with an "edgy" redesigned decal set, a "sportier" meter cluster and a "sportier" muffler. — [Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html) (confirmed in search summary of [Manila Bulletin, 30 Nov 2023](https://mb.com.ph/2023/11/30/suzuki-introduces-smash-fi-leisure-underbone), page itself 403)
- Visible features: engine heat guard protecting the rider's legs, shutter key lock, 7.6 L underseat storage, 3.7 L fuel tank. Wire-wheel (spoke) base version and alloy-wheel (disc brake) version. — [Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html)

### Inferences
- Both bikes are "underbone" silhouettes: a low, step-through-style open section between the seat and a faired front end, with the engine as a stressed/hanging element under a backbone tube. For modelling, the Smash is the smaller, rounder, more commuter-like form (shorter, 99 kg class) and the Raider is the longer, more raked, sportier form with larger front fairing and disc brakes at both ends.
- The MY2026 Raider's silhouette is probably a mild evolution of the MY2017 to MY2025 bike (same wheelbase, same 17" wheels, same dimensions class) with restyled panels and winglets, and the Pro's headlight is the one big front-end difference. (Inferred from the identical wheelbase and tire sizes and from the "keeps familiar headlamp" wording for Blade.)
- The Smash Fi is a decal/cluster/muffler refresh of the Smash 115 body, not a new body; the same 3D body shell can serve both with different decals.

### Gaps
- No text source describes the tank shape, side-cover shape, tail-light outline, fender shape, or legshield for either bike. Photos were not viewable. A modeller should check Suzuki PH product images (mc.suzuki.com.ph) directly.
- Winglet size and exact placement on the Raider (front fairing, side of the headlight is likely) are only described generally.
- Whether the Blade Edition head/tail lights are the same units as the previous-gen Fi is unconfirmed.
- Whether the Smash Fi headlight shape changed vs the Smash 115 is not stated; Suzuki PH says the look is "retained".

## 2. Key dimensions per spec sheets

### Takeaway
Raider R150 Fi: about 1,960 to 1,995 mm long, 675 mm wide, 980 mm high, wheelbase 1,280 mm, seat 765 mm, ground clearance 150 mm, 17" wheels with 70/90 front and 80/90 rear, 107 to 115 kg depending on source and trim. Smash 115: about 1,930 x 655 x 1,040 mm, wheelbase 1,240 mm, ground clearance 130 mm, 70/90-17 front and 80/90-17 rear, 99 kg. Sources conflict on some Smash values (fuel tank, seat height, ground clearance for the Fi).

### Gaps

## 3. Wheels and tires, brakes, fork/rear shock, exhaust/muffler, engine appearance

### Takeaway
Raider: cast alloy mags (17"), petal/wavy discs at both ends (2-piston front caliper), telescopic fork, rear mono-shock, aggressive muffler, liquid-cooled DOHC engine. Smash: spoked wheels with drum brakes (base) or cast mags with front disc (higher variant), telescopic fork, rear swingarm with coil-over, large 100 mm muffler, air-cooled engine with a heat guard. Rim colours are known for the Raider only via the 2022 colour set; exhaust finish is poorly documented for both.

### Cited Findings

**Raider R150 Fi**
- Cast alloy wheels, 17" front and rear (MY2026 Blade/Pro). — [Top Gear PH 2025](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/suzuki-raider-r150-2026-launch-a5361-20251117)
- Blade Edition has two-piston front caliper and petal discs on both wheels. — [Premiumbikes.ph](https://premiumbikes.ph/product/suzuki-raider-r150-blade-edition/)
- Previous Fi was described as having a "unique wavy disc brake". — [Top Gear PH 2017](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-review/review-suzuki-raider-r150-fi-a1565-20171106)
- Wikipedia: front single disc with 2-piston caliper; rear single disc with 1-piston caliper; rear is a steel swingarm with mono-shock; front is a telescopic fork. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_Raider_150)
- Top Gear PH: telescopic forks in front, mono-shock at the rear (Fi, MY2017). — [Top Gear PH 2017](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-review/review-suzuki-raider-r150-fi-a1565-20171106)
- Rim colour by colour (MY2022): Stellar Blue has black mags; Titan Black has red mags; Pearl Bright Ivory has red mags; Candy Matte Bordeaux Red has black mags. — [CarGuide.PH, Apr 2022](https://www.carguide.ph/2022/04/suzuki-raider-r150-fi-adds-new-colors.html)

- Engine: 147 cc, liquid-cooled, DOHC 4-valve single (same engine as GSX-R150 per Wikipedia), 6-speed. MY2026 adds a slipper clutch (Suzuki Clutch Assist System) and Suzuki Eco Performance. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_Raider_150); [Top Gear PH 2025](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-feature/suzuki-raider-r150-2026-launch-a5361-20251117)

**Smash 115 / Smash Fi**
- Smash 115 spoke version: front and rear drum brakes. Cast "mags" version: front disc, rear drum. Tires 70/90-17 front, 80/90-17 rear. — [suzukicycles.org](https://suzukicycles.org/Smash/Smash-Fi-115.shtml); [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/suzuki/smash-115) (Zigwheels: "lacks alloy wheels" on the page, which conflicts with the existence of a mags version; the Disc variant has a front disc, rear drum, the Drum variant has drum at both ends)
- Smash Fi: wire wheels on the base version, alloy wheels with disc brake on the higher version. — [Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html)
- Suspension: front telescopic with coil spring and oil damping; rear swing arm with coil spring and oil damping. — [suzukicycles.org](https://suzukicycles.org/Smash/Smash-Fi-115.shtml)
- Exhaust: large-diameter 100 mm muffler with an oxidation catalyzer (Smash 115); the Smash Fi muffler is called "sporty". — [Top Gear PH Smash launch](https://www.topgear.com.ph/news/suzuki-philippines-launches-all-new-smash-115); [Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html)
- Engine: 113 cc air-cooled single, 4-speed; the Smash Fi has an engine heat guard covering the leg-side of the engine/exhaust. — [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/suzuki/smash-115); [Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html)

### Inferences
- A faithful Raider wheel is a multi-spoke cast mag with a black or red face depending on colour (MY2022 pattern); MY2026 rim colours are likely black or silver-black by analogy but were not confirmed.
- A "petal" (wavy-edge) disc at each end is a quick recognition cue on the Raider; a plain-looking drum hub or a single front disc is the Smash cue.
- Smash rear suspension: sources say "swing arm with coil spring and oil damping" without a count. Underbones in this class commonly use twin rear shocks, but no fetched source confirms this for the Smash. Flag as a modeller's assumption to check against photos.

### Gaps
- Rear shock count for the Smash not stated. Raider rear is stated as a mono-shock (Top Gear, Wikipedia), and mounting (offset/cantilever) not described.
- Raider MY2026 wheel/rim colours and exhaust/muffler shape and finish: not found in text sources.
- Smash spoke-wheel rim finish (chrome vs painted) and mags design: not found.
- Engine cover and cooling-fin detail for both: only liquid-cooled (Raider) vs air-cooled (Smash) established.

## 6. Distinctive features a modeller must get right for instant recognition

### Takeaway
Raider: sharp, forward-leaning faired front end with winglets (MY2026), LED lamps, petal/wavy discs at both ends, 17" cast mags, long low seat, digital cluster; Pro's wider Gixxer-style headlamp. Smash: GSX-R-inspired fairing with a wide multi-reflector headlight, sports-watch speedo pod, fat 100 mm muffler, and a spoke-wheel/drum base or mags/front-disc upper version.

### Cited Findings
- Raider: plane-inspired winglets on the front fairing (MY2026), full-LED head and tail lights, one-action key shutter, Pro's Gixxer-like wide LED headlamp. — [Lifestyle On Wheels](https://lifestyleonwheels.com/raider-r150-blade-long-live-the-king/); [Two and Four PH](https://www.twoandfour.ph/2026/sharpening-the-king-the-suzuki-raider-r150-blade-edition/); [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/suzuki-ph-launches-raider-r150-pro-blade-edition)
- Raider: petal discs both ends with 2-piston front caliper; wavy disc noted in MY2017. — [Premiumbikes.ph](https://premiumbikes.ph/product/suzuki-raider-r150-blade-edition/); [Top Gear PH 2017](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-review/review-suzuki-raider-r150-fi-a1565-20171106)
- Raider: seat height 765 mm (30.1 in) is high for an underbone; low handlebar; wide long seat. — [Top Gear PH 2017](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-review/review-suzuki-raider-r150-fi-a1565-20171106)
- Smash: GSX-R-style front fairing, multi-reflector headlight, multi-polygon-reflector tail lamp, sports-watch speedo with gear indicator, 100 mm muffler, magnetic key shutter, engine heat guard (Fi). — [Top Gear PH Smash launch](https://www.topgear.com.ph/news/suzuki-philippines-launches-all-new-smash-115); [Motopinas](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-smash-fi-starts-at-php-66-400.html)

### Inferences
- Recognition cues in priority order. Raider: (1) faired sharp nose and lamp, (2) winglets on the MY2026 body, (3) disc at both ends with petal discs, (4) 17" mags in contrasting colour, (5) low long seat sloping to a pointed tail. Smash: (1) rounder GSX-R-style nose with a wide headlight, (2) spoke wheels with drums on the base version, (3) large muffler, (4) simple two-tone graphics.
- A Raider that is 1,960 to 1,995 mm long with a 1,280 mm wheelbase and a Smash that is 1,930 mm long with a 1,240 mm wheelbase differ by only about 40 mm in wheelbase, so the silhouettes are close in length. Height (980 vs 1,040 mm) and the Smash's taller, more upright seating are the proportion differences.

### Gaps
- No source states the tail-section silhouette of either bike (pointed vs blunt, grab-rail shape). Exhaust finish for the Raider unknown.

## 7. Model-year differences that change the look

### Takeaway
Raider: generation changes in 2003, 2008, 2013, 2016 (Fi, LED, digital), then the November 2025 (MY2026) 5th-gen with winglets/new panels and the Pro's new headlamp, plus the Shadow Edition in October 2026. Smash: 110 replaced by 115, a new colour in 2019, then the Fi in November 2023 (MY2024) with new decals/cluster/muffler.

### Cited Findings
- Raider global history: 1st gen 2003 to 2008 (oil-cooled carb, launched in PH 2004); 2008 facelift changed headlight design; 2013 facelift new headlight bodywork, sharper style; 2016 2nd generation with digital speedometer, LED headlights, fuel injection and liquid cooling. — [Wikipedia: Suzuki Raider 150](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki_Raider_150)
- Raider PH: MY2017 Fi got a new headlight and more aggressive muffler (Top Gear test, Nov 2017); MY2019 Fi and Carb sold side by side (Fi in Blue, Red, Black); MY2020 MotoGP livery on the Carb (headline only); MY2021 new liveries; MY2022 new colour set; 2025 20th-anniversary Raider colours. — [Top Gear PH 2017](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-review/review-suzuki-raider-r150-fi-a1565-20171106); [Top Gear PH guide](https://www.topgear.com.ph/index.php/buyers-guide/motorcycles/suzuki/raider-r150); [Top Gear PH, Sep 2020](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2020-suzuki-raider-r150-motogp-src-topbikes-a4696-20200921); [Motopinas 2021](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/suzuki-unveils-new-colors-for-raider-r150.html); [CarGuide.PH 2022](https://www.carguide.ph/2022/04/suzuki-raider-r150-fi-adds-new-colors.html); [Wheeltek](https://wheeltek.com.ph/motorcycles/raider-r150-fi/)

### Inferences
- If the game models one Raider, the MY2026 Blade Edition is the "current" bike (winglets, new panels, the familiar headlamp); a Pro would need the wider Gixxer-style headlamp. A MY2017 to MY2025 Raider Fi would be the older headlamp and panels, and its colours are the MY2022 set.
- If the game models one Smash, the Smash Fi (MY2024 to MY2025) is the current bike; it has the same body as the 2019 Smash 115 with new decals and a heat guard.
