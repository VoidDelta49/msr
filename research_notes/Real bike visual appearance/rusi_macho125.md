# Rusi Macho 125 (TC 125): real-world visual appearance, for a 3D model in "CPM: Motor Edition"

Research date: 2026-11-06. Method: web search and page fetches plus direct visual inspection of the product photos hosted by Kamote.ph (downloaded and viewed). **No source gives a model year for the Macho 125**; Kamote/Bikes4sale/BikeDunia pages are undated and the only dated item is Yugatech's "2025-2026" Rusi price list (found only as a search snippet; the page itself returned HTTP 403). The official Rusi site (rusi.com.ph) was unreachable (HTTP 503 via WebFetch, TLS failure via curl), so **no manufacturer primary source was read**. Everything below is from third-party listing sites and photos. Sources are thin; gaps are flagged.

Naming note found in the evidence: the Macho line is badged "TC" on the bodywork. The Macho 125 photo shows decals reading "RUSI TC 125" with the Rusi "R" logo ([Kamote photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_125.webp)); the 175 photo reads "R MACHO TC-175" ([Kamote photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_175.webp)); the 150 photo reads "TC 150" ([Kamote photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_150.webp)); aftermarket decal sellers sell "Rusi Macho TC 125 / 150 / 175" sets ([TikTok Shop PH](https://shop-ph.tiktok.com/pdp/decals-for-rusi-macho-tc-125-150-175-by-tuni-graphix-premium-glossy/1730230027941088139)). So "Macho" and "TC" are the same family.

Important caveat on the visual evidence: all "what it looks like" findings below come from ONE side-on studio photo of the 125 (black colourway, bike facing right, so its RIGHT side is shown). The other colourways (red, blue) were not seen. Observations marked "photo" are my own reading of the image, not statements by the sources.

## Overall silhouette and body design language (frame, tank, headlight, tail light, side covers, seat, rack, fenders, proportions)

### Takeaway
The Macho 125 is a classic Honda-CG-style "standard"/utility bike: tall-ish 17" spoked wheels, a slim knee-notched steel tank, a long flat black seat, a black spine ("backbone") frame, a chrome rear grab-rack/carrier, a long low chrome exhaust and an exposed air-cooled engine, plus a distinctive angular black headlight shroud (mini-fairing) in the only photo available. Sources agree it is a no-frills commuter/workhorse, described as the budget "Chinese copy of the TMX 125".

### Cited Findings
- Frame type is "Backbone" for the Macho 125 (the Macho 175 is listed "Double cradle") — [Kamote Macho 125](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125); [Kamote Macho 175](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-175)
- Kamote's blog calls the Macho 125 "the Chinese copy of the TMX 125 but more affordable", says its "general design" keeps price at the lowest economical level (₱42,500 SRP) and calls it "perfect for custom builds" — [Kamote blog](https://kamote.ph/blog/top-7-rusi-motorcycles)
- BikeDunia says the styling "stays true to basic commuter lines", familiar and functional, with a backbone frame — [BikeDunia](https://www.bikedunia.com/motorcycles/rusi/macho/macho-125/)
- Lighting: halogen headlamp and halogen tail lamp, analog display — [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125); [Kamote Macho 125](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125)
- Fuel tank 9.5 L (Macho 150 and 175 are 12 L, so those have visibly bigger tanks or different tank shapes) — [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125); [Kamote Macho 150](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-150)
- The model it is said to copy, Honda TMX 125 Alpha, is "essentially a rebadged Honda CG125" (OHV engine, backbone frame, 18" wire wheels, drum brakes) and its 2021 update used matte silver with fewer decals; the TMX line began as a tricycle-oriented heavy-duty derivative of the Honda S110 — [Wikipedia: Honda TMX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX); [Kamote TMX125 Alpha](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/honda-tmx125-alpha)
- Photo observations of the Macho 125 (black version) — [Kamote Macho 125 photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_125.webp):
  - Fuel tank: slim, rectangular "classic" steel tank with a flat top, knee scallops at the lower rear flank, tapering to the steering head; round filler cap on top, roughly centred, with a blue/teal cap or ring.
  - Seat: long, flat, black, essentially one continuous slab (rider and pillion sections; tiny step up at the rear), vinyl, no visible grab strap; seat latch visible low at the front underside.
  - Rear carrier: chrome tubular carrier at the tail (flat rail across the tail, bent-tube sides) that continues forward as a chrome rail running along both sides of the seat, ending beside the rider's section, with small triangular/diamond tube bracing in the side panel. This is a prominent, instantly recognisable feature.
  - Tail light: small rectangular red lens on a short black bracket under the rear carrier at the very back; small amber rear turn signals on stalks below the carrier (an orange lens is visible at the rear underside).
  - Side cover: black rectangular battery/tool cover under the seat with a vented/louvred top-rear edge and the yellow "RUSI TC 125" decal with the R logo.
  - Front fender: long chrome (metal) fender with a chrome stay, hugging the tyre and extending down at the front and back of the wheel.
  - Rear fender: short and mostly hidden under the rack (a short black mudguard/bracket at the tail); no big rear fender.
  - Headlight: an angular black plastic shroud/cowl (mini-fairing, flat sloping front with a squared-off top edge) wrapping the headlamp and the top of the fork, substantially taller and wider than a plain round CG lamp; small orange round reflector on the fork ear.
  - Frame: black tubular spine frame; black triangular engine-mounting plates; engine and cylinder fully exposed (no belly pan, no engine covers other than the silver cases).
- Siblings by photo ([Macho 150](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_150.webp), [Macho 175](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_175.webp)): both are the same CG-style layout; the 150 shows a black/white/silver graphics set, a black rear rack rail and a notably stepped seat with a raised pillion section; the 175 shows a black tank with red-white stripes, a ribbed black seat, no rack seen, and a small black angular headlamp housing. The 125 photo is the only one with the large black headlight shroud and the chrome side-rail rack.

### Inferences
- Visual lineage is Honda CG125 → Honda TMX 125 Alpha → Chinese clone (Rusi "TC" series). For a model, the TMX Alpha is a good reference for tank/seat/frame/engine stance, but the Macho differs in the headlight shroud (TMX has a rectangular lamp with separate indicators on stalks), 17" instead of 18" wheels, the chrome fender and chrome side-rail rack.
- With an exposed single-cylinder engine, a spine frame and no body panels, the model needs only these separate bodies: tank, seat, side covers, headlight shroud, chrome rack, fenders and exhaust.

### Gaps
- No official Rusi product page or brochure could be read, so the photo of record (Kamote) may not match the current production bike. Kamote's gallery image is not captioned with model year or a colourway.
- No second independent photo of the Macho 125 (front 3/4, rear 3/4, left side) was found, so left-side details (kick lever, gear lever, battery cover on the other side, chain guard, instrument panel layout, front view of the lamp) are unverified.
- Whether the Macho 125 headlight shroud is stock on all colours and years or specific to certain batches is unknown.

## Key dimensions (length, width, height, wheelbase, seat height, ground clearance, wheel/tyre sizes, weight)

### Takeaway
Wheelbase 1,260 mm, seat height 760 mm, ground clearance 160 mm, kerb weight 104 kg, tyres 2.75-17 front / 3.00-17 rear, 9.5 L tank. Overall length, width and height are NOT published in any source found.

### Cited Findings
- Wheelbase 1,260 mm; seat height 760 mm; ground clearance 160 mm; curb weight 104 kg; tyres front 2.75-17, rear 3-17; length/width/height listed as not available — [Kamote Macho 125](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125)
- The same figures (1,260 / 760 / 160 / 104 kg, 2.75-17 and 3-17, 9.5 L) appear on BikeDunia, with length, width and height also not listed — [BikeDunia](https://www.bikedunia.com/motorcycles/rusi/macho/macho-125/)
- Bikes4sale lists 17" spoke wheels and a 9.5 L tank but gives no dimensions or weight — [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125)
- Sibling comparison: Macho 175 = 110 kg, 775 mm seat, 140 mm clearance, 1,270 mm wheelbase, 2.75-17 / 3-17, 12 L tank — [Kamote Macho 175](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-175). Macho 150 = 2.75-17 / 3-17, 12 L tank, no dimensions listed — [Kamote Macho 150](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-150)
- Reference proxy, Honda TMX125 Alpha: L 1,904 / W 754 / H 1,026 mm, wheelbase 1,226 mm, seat 759 mm, clearance 156 mm, 113 kg, 2.50-18 / 2.75-18 tyres, 8.6 L tank — [Kamote TMX125 Alpha](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/honda-tmx125-alpha)
- Price (context only): ₱42,500 on Kamote and the Yugatech 2025-2026 Rusi price list (per search snippet), ₱42,000 on Bikes4sale, "₱40,000" in one sentence of the Kamote blog — [Kamote Macho 125](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125); [Yugatech](https://moto.yugatech.com/news/rusi-motorcycle-price-list-in-the-philippines-2025-2026/); [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125); [Kamote blog](https://kamote.ph/blog/top-7-rusi-motorcycles)

### Inferences
- BikeDunia and Kamote carry identical numbers, so they are probably one data lineage (likely BikeDunia copied Kamote or both used one Rusi spec sheet); treat them as ONE source, not two confirmations.
- My own scaling of the Kamote side photo (using the 1,260 mm wheelbase as the scale, hub-to-hub about 925 px; rough, eyeballed, single photo) gives overall length roughly 1.95-2.0 m including the rack, and height to the top of the headlight shroud/mirror stem roughly 1.05-1.10 m. These are estimates only, not published figures, but they land close to the TMX Alpha's published 1,904 x 1,026 mm, so they look plausible.
- Rough proportions from that photo (estimates): wheel centre roughly 0.29-0.30 m above ground (consistent with a 17" wheel plus 2.75/3.00 section tyre, outer diameter about 0.57-0.60 m); seat top line visibly higher than the quoted 760 mm because the quoted figure is likely measured at the front/lowest part of the seat; tank sits roughly at seat level, the front of the tank is level with the top of the fork bellows; the muffler's rear end extends past the rear axle.
- Overall "feel": heavier and longer-wheelbase than a small 110 cc underbone; taller than a scooter; a CG-sized bike with a slightly longer wheelbase than the TMX Alpha (1,260 vs 1,226 mm) and 1 inch smaller wheels.

### Gaps
- Overall length/width/height not found in any source (Rusi official specs not accessible). Handlebar width and exact seat length also not found.
- No independent spec confirmation beyond one data lineage; no model-year attribution for the spec values.

## Wheel and tyre look, brakes, fork and shocks, exhaust and engine appearance

### Takeaway
Wire-spoke 17" chrome-rim wheels with silver drum brakes front and rear, telescopic fork with rubber boots and chrome sliders, twin chrome-sprung rear shocks, a long chrome straight-through muffler with a sweeping chrome header, and an exposed silver air-cooled 125 cc single with deep vertical cooling fins, electric plus kick start.

### Cited Findings
- Wheels: wire spoke, 17"; brakes drum front and rear; ABS not applicable — [Kamote Macho 125](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125); [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125)
- Suspension: telescopic fork at the front; dual-spring rear shock absorbers (Bikes4sale) — [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125). Kamote lists "telescopic" for both ends (almost certainly a data-entry shortcut) — [Kamote Macho 125](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125)
- Engine: 125 cc single-cylinder, 4-stroke, air-cooled, 2-valve SOHC, carburettor, CDI ignition, 5-speed manual, electric plus kick start — [Kamote Macho 125](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125); [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125)
- Photo observations ([Kamote Macho 125 photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_125.webp)):
  - Wheels: thin wire spokes (chrome/zinc), chrome steel rims; fat black tyres; rear tyre shows a transverse-groove "rib and lug" style pattern; front hub is a small, full-width silver-painted drum with a central cap and a ring of rivets; rear drum similar with a brake arm and a torque rod; a yellow "RUSI" lettering runs vertically down the right front fork leg.
  - Front fork: chrome upper tubes with black ribbed rubber gaiters (bellows boots) over the lower section, chrome lower sliders, orange round reflector on the fork ear, brake cable and speedo cable visible on the right of the fork.
  - Rear shocks: two exposed coil springs, chrome springs with chrome upper covers and black damper bodies, mounted at a slight forward lean; short travel.
  - Exhaust: chrome header pipe leaves the cylinder head forward, runs down in front of the engine and sweeps back into a long, slightly tapered cylindrical chrome muffler along the right side, low, finishing level with or just past the rear axle, with a flat-cut end (no upswept tip, no heat shield, a cross-hanger bracket at the front half).
  - Engine: silver-painted aluminium; vertical cylinder tilted slightly forward with a tall, deep, closely-spaced fin stack on the barrel and head; carburettor on the rear side; a teardrop/rounded-trapezoid right crankcase cover with an embossed "R" logo; black engine plates and black swingarm with yellow "RFI" lettering along the right swingarm; a stand with a return spring under the engine (type unverified).
  - Footpegs: black rider pegs on engine-side brackets; chrome pillion peg bracket plates beneath the rack at the rear.

### Inferences
- The engine silhouette in the photo is the standard CG-style tall-finned single (SOHC here versus the OHV Honda original); for a model, copy the CG125 engine look (head, cylinder fins, right-side cover, carb).
- Rim colour: chrome/bright silver with chrome spokes; hubs silver (not black).
- The shocks and fender mean a lot of chrome on the rear half: rack, springs, fender, muffler, header.

### Gaps
- No reliable source for rim section (WM/WL) or tyre brand/pattern; the tyre pattern is a photo reading only.
- Kick lever, gear lever, left crankcase cover, chain guard, and chain are not visible in the one photo (right side).
- Stand type (centre vs side) not verified.

## Instrument cluster, handlebar, mirrors, footpegs and controls

### Takeaway
Sources only confirm an "analog" speedometer and halogen lighting. Details of the cluster, switches and mirrors come only from one photo and are limited.

### Cited Findings
- Analog display; halogen headlamp and tail lamp; electric plus kick start — [Kamote Macho 125](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-125); [Bikes4sale](https://www.bikes4sale.com/details/rusi/macho/macho-125)
- Photo observations ([Kamote Macho 125 photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_125.webp)): wide, slightly swept, black tubular handlebar with black grips and black levers; the cluster is not visible (tucked inside the black headlight shroud); a thin black mirror stalk rises above the bar on the right, but no mirror glass is visible in the photo (it may be missing or cut out of the image); switch cubes appear black.
- On the siblings, the photos show a rounded rectangular mirror on a stalk (the 175) and two mirrors (the 150) — [Macho 175 photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_175.webp); [Macho 150 photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_150.webp)
- Reference: the Honda TMX 125 Alpha has an analog tachometer with a gear indicator, bar-mounted kill switch, bar-end weights, and a round-corner rectangular headlight with the indicators on stalks — [Wikipedia: Honda TMX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX); [Kamote TMX125 Alpha photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Honda/TMX125_Alpha.webp)

### Inferences
- A single analog speedometer (probably with a combined odometer and basic indicator lights, as on Chinese CG-type bikes) sits inside or just behind the headlight shroud. The exact layout is unknown; model it as a simple round or rectangular analog pod hidden mostly behind the shroud.
- Mirrors are normally fitted on a bike of this type; use two round-rectangle mirrors with short black stalks.

### Gaps
- No cluster photo, switchgear detail, or mirror shape was found for the Macho 125. Whether it has a tachometer or gear indicator is not documented.

## Distinctive recognition features, base design, siblings and model-year/variant differences

### Takeaway
Instant-recognition cues: CG-style standard bike in black with silver-white swoosh and gold bars, bold yellow "RUSI" lettering, a big angular black headlight shroud, chrome side-rail rear carrier, chrome front fender, chrome-sprung twin shocks, and a long chrome muffler with sweeping header. Rusi's Macho family is a three-size TC range (125, 150, 175) built on the same CG-style layout.

### Cited Findings
- Family: Macho 125 (₱42,500), Macho 150 (12 L tank; price/displacement ₱45,000 and 144.6 cc per a search-result summary of Kamote, not re-verified on the page), Macho 175 (12 L; 110 kg; double-cradle frame; price ₱48,500 per a search-result summary, not re-verified) — [Kamote Rusi list](https://www.kamote.ph/Rusi); [Kamote Macho 150](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-150); [Kamote Macho 175](https://www.kamote.ph/motorcycle/rusi-macho-175)
- Macho 125 is described as the cheapest and most beginner-friendly Rusi, "perfect for custom builds", and a "Chinese copy of the TMX 125" — [Kamote blog](https://kamote.ph/blog/top-7-rusi-motorcycles)
- The Honda TMX line that the Macho imitates began as the TM 110, a heavy-duty derivative of the Honda S110 built for tricycle use (no source found says the Rusi Macho 125 itself is marketed for tricycles) — [Wikipedia: Honda TMX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honda_TMX)
- Visual differences between the 125 and the 150/175 in the photos: the 125 has the large black headlight shroud and chrome side-rail rack; the 150 has a stepped seat and a larger 12 L tank; the 175 has a ribbed seat, a larger tank, and a taller-looking build (110 kg, 775 mm seat, 1,270 mm wheelbase, 140 mm clearance) — [Macho 125 photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_125.webp); [Macho 150 photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_150.webp); [Macho 175 photo](https://www.kamote.ph/Gallery/Rusi/Macho_175.webp)
- Rusi's 2025-2026 price list still lists "Macho 125, 125cc carburetor, P42,500" per search-result summary of the Yugatech page — [Yugatech](https://moto.yugatech.com/news/rusi-motorcycle-price-list-in-the-philippines-2025-2026/)

### Inferences
- A modeller should get these right first (in order of recognisability): (1) CG-style tall-finned silver single, exposed, with a rounded-trapezoid right cover with an "R"; (2) 17" chrome spoked wheels with small silver drums; (3) black slim tank with silver swoosh + gold bars + yellow "RUSI"; (4) chrome rack with side rails; (5) chrome front fender; (6) long chrome muffler with tapered body; (7) chrome-spring twin shocks; (8) angular black headlight shroud.
- Because the game's existing bike data already sets a TMX cluster and 1.26 m wheelbase/104 kg/760 mm for the Macho 125, the dimensions are already consistent with the sources; the points to verify in the model are the shroud, the rack and the colours.
- Variant/model-year differences cannot be stated: no source distinguishes model years of the Macho 125.

### Gaps
- No data on model-year changes (graphics, colours, shroud, rack), nor on whether the Macho 125 was ever sold with a different headlight or with cast wheels or disc brakes.
- Source thinness: all spec data trace to Kamote (and a likely copy on BikeDunia); the only visual reference is one Kamote photo whose decals say "TC 125". A dealer photo set, Rusi's Facebook page, or the official site (rusi.com.ph, not reachable from this environment) would be the next step.
- One search tool summary wrongly called the TMX a Yamaha; Wikipedia and Honda Philippines data confirm it is a Honda.
