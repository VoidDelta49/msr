# Real bike visual appearance: Kawasaki Rouser NS125 Fi (Philippines) and Kawasaki Ninja H2 (standard supercharged)

Scope note: appearance and dimensions only. Research date 2026-11-06. Source quality for the Rouser is weak: Kawasaki Philippines (kawasaki.com.ph) returned HTTP 503 and the Bajaj Philippines page redirected to a page without the model, so Rouser data comes from aggregators (ZigWheels PH, Carmudi PH), 2020 launch articles (Top Gear PH, Inside Racing) and the Indian Bajaj Pulsar NS125, which the Rouser is reported to be a rebadge of. The Ninja H2 has a strong primary source (Kawasaki USA 2025 spec sheets).

## 1. Overall silhouette and body design language

### Takeaway
Rouser NS125 Fi: a small, naked, sporty commuter derived from the Bajaj Pulsar NS family. Pulsar NS125 styling has a wolf-eye twin-pilot headlamp, perimeter frame, split seat and split grab rail, and twin-strip LED tail lamps. Ninja H2: an aerodynamic hypersport with a full fairing, a mono-headlight tucked into the middle of the nose, a green steel trellis frame, a single-sided swingarm and a mirror-finish black paint.

### Cited Findings
**Rouser NS125 Fi**
- Launched in the Philippines in 2020 at P63,000 as a "sleek and edgy" bike aimed at standing out among scooters and underbones. It comes in Sparkling Black or Bright Yellow, with a semi-digital panel (analog tachometer, digital display, FI indicator). — [Top Gear PH, 2020](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2020-kawasaki-ns125-fi-a959-20200622)
- ZigWheels describes it as essentially a rebadged Bajaj Pulsar NS125 from India. A reviewer says it keeps the design language of the larger NS160/NS200, more than a decade old, with a clip-on handlebar, a small visor, a semi-digital instrument panel and a fuel tank carried over. Kawasaki added new paint schemes and graphics. The review says the look now seems somewhat dated and the bike has no LED headlamp. — [ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi)
- Global Bajaj Philippines' Rouser NS125 FI listing (as summarised by a search result, page not directly fetchable) mentions twin-pilot headlamps, a split seat with split rear grab rail, and a ventilated front disc. — [Global Bajaj PH](https://philippines.globalbajaj.com/en/Brands/Rouser/Rouser-NS125-FI)
- Bajaj Pulsar NS125 (India, launched 2021): "wolf eyed" headlamp cluster with twin pilot lamps, split grab rail, twin strip LED tail lamps, rigid perimeter frame, telescopic fork, Nitrox mono-shock, 17-inch bronze-coloured alloy wheels, high-gloss metallic body, styling similar to the NS200. — [Rushlane](https://www.rushlane.com/bajaj-pulsar-ns-125-first-look-walkaround-exhaust-note-12400400.html)
- Kawasaki PH lists the current Rouser NS125 Fi with a halogen adjustable headlight, LED tail light, front disc / rear drum brake, a telescopic front fork, a rear swingarm (monoshock not stated), a digital odometer and digital fuel gauge, and a 10.5 L tank. — [ZigWheels PH specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi/specifications)
- Price listed at P82,000 for the single Standard variant, shown for 2025/2026. — [ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi), [Carmudi PH](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi)

**Ninja H2**
- Styling concept "Intense Force Design"; the paint is hand-applied with twice the normal number of coats and a layer of genuine silver for a mirror finish; first Kawasaki tubular steel trellis frame, finished in bright green. — [Auto Trader UK](https://www.autotrader.co.uk/bikes/content/kawasaki-ninja-h2-review-super-sports-2014) (as summarised by search result)
- The street H2 has mirrors where the track-only H2R has winglets, and plastic body panels in place of the H2R's carbon-fibre. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawasaki_Ninja_H2)
- Mono-headlight tucked into the middle of the front fairing; seat tapers at the front so the bike feels narrow; tail unit adjusts into three positions to alter the side bolsters; candy green trellis frame; single-sided swingarm. H2R has carbon front and side fairings and winglets; the H2 has stainless exhaust, the H2R titanium. — [Visordown first ride](https://www.visordown.com/reviews/first-ride/first-ride-kawasaki-ninja-h2-and-h2r-review)
- Bodywork very aerodynamic like the H2R's to add downforce; LED headlamps, turn signals and tail lights; the Mirror Coated Black finish looks black in shade and silver in bright light, much of it hand-painted; the street bike gets "larger and quieter" street pipes, with a noticeably larger right-side muffler than the H2R; trellis frame is high-tensile steel painted green; five-spoke cast aluminium wheels; riding position similar to a ZX-10R but less aggressive. — [Cycle News first look, 2015 model](https://www.cyclenews.com/2014/11/article/first-look-2015-kawasaki-ninja-h2-street/)
- Single-sided swingarm described as a Kawasaki first on a street model; it lets the right-side silencer tuck in close to the frame. — [Bennetts / Bike Social](https://www.bennetts.co.uk/bikesocial/news-and-views/news/archive/kawasaki-ninja-h2-full-spec-and-photos), [IOL](https://iol.co.za/motoring/bikes/2014-12-12-kawasaki-takes-wraps-off-new-h2/) (via search-result summaries)
- Trellis frame, high-tensile steel, with a swingarm mounting plate. Special features: supercharged engine, all-digital TFT colour instrumentation, all-LED lighting, highly durable paint. — [Kawasaki USA 2025 H2 spec sheet](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs)

### Inferences
- The Rouser NS125 Fi is very likely the Pulsar NS125 body, so Pulsar NS125 photos are a reasonable shape reference. This rests on ZigWheels' "rebadge" statement plus matching descriptions; no direct Kawasaki statement was found.
- The Philippine Rouser appears to be a simpler-spec version than the Indian Pulsar NS125 (halogen headlamp, drum rear brake). Treat Indian LED/Bluetooth variants as not applicable.

### Gaps
- No primary Kawasaki PH description of the Rouser NS125 Fi body (kawasaki.com.ph returned 503).
- Not sourced: exact tank-extension shape, side-cover shape, rear fender/hugger and tail-lamp shape for the Rouser; would need photos. Use ZigWheels gallery (10 exterior images: right side, fuel tank, engine, rear suspension, seat, silencer, front/rear brake, front/rear tyre) at https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi/images
- Not sourced for H2: the exact tail light shape and tail section layout; whether the street H2 has small fixed aero fins on the fairing (the sources only say mirrors replace the H2R winglets).
- Wikipedia page was summarised by the fetch tool and not read verbatim; verify before quoting.

## 2. Key dimensions

### Takeaway
Ninja H2 (2025, Kawasaki USA): 2085 x 770 x 1125 mm, wheelbase 1455 mm, seat 826 mm, ground clearance 130 mm, 17-inch wheels with 120/70-17 front and 200/55-17 rear, curb weight 238 kg. The Rouser's official Philippine dimensions were not found; the Indian Pulsar NS125 is the best proxy (2012 x 810 x 1078 mm, wheelbase 1353 mm, 17-inch wheels).

### Cited Findings
**Ninja H2 (2025 model, Kawasaki USA)**
- Length 82.1 in, width 30.3 in, height 44.3 in, wheelbase 57.3 in, seat 32.5 in, ground clearance 5.1 in, rake 24.5 deg, trail 4.1 in, curb weight 524.8 lb, estimated dry weight 480.7 lb, fuel capacity 4.5 gal. Tyres 120/70-17 and 200/55-17. Front: dual 330 mm semi-floating discs with radial-mount opposed 4-piston Brembo Stylema calipers. Rear: single 250 mm disc with 2-piston caliper. Front fork 43 mm inverted KYB AOS-II, rear Uni-Trak with Ohlins TTX36 gas shock with piggyback reservoir. — [Kawasaki USA 2025 H2 spec sheet](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs)
- Metric figures (as listed): wheelbase 1455 mm, length 2085 mm, width 770 mm, height 1125 mm, seat height 826 mm, ground clearance 130 mm, trail 104 mm, travel 119 mm front / 135 mm rear, fuel 17.0 L, dry weight 218 kg, wet weight 238 kg. Metal fuel tank. — [MotorcycleDB 2025 H2](https://www.motorcycledb.com/Ninja_H2_2025/68606)
- H2 Carbon (2025) has the same dimensions, weights and tyres as the base H2. — [Kawasaki USA 2025 H2 Carbon spec sheet](https://kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-carbon-abs)
- H2R (2025), for contrast: length 81.5 in, width 33.5 in, height 45.7 in, wheelbase 57.1 in, seat 32.7 in, curb weight 476.3 lb, slick tyres 120/60-17 and 190/65-17, same 330 mm / 250 mm brake discs. — [Kawasaki USA 2025 H2R spec sheet](https://kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2r-abs)
- H2 SX (2018 on): larger fuel tank, rear trellis subframe, panniers, claimed wet weight 256.1 kg. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawasaki_Ninja_H2)

**Rouser NS125 Fi (Philippines)**
- Fuel tank 10.5 L (spec page) or 10.2 L (review text, ZigWheels); 240 mm ventilated front disc; rear drum; chain drive; two seats. — [ZigWheels PH specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi/specifications), [ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi), [Inside Racing 2020](https://insideracing.com.ph/?p=49250)
- 2020 launch coverage says it has the lowest seat height among 125 cc standard motorcycles (no figure given). — [Top Gear PH, 2020](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2020-kawasaki-ns125-fi-a959-20200622)

**Proxy: Bajaj Pulsar NS125 (India)**
- Length 2012 mm, width 810 mm, height 1078 mm, wheelbase 1353 mm, seat 805 mm, ground clearance 179 mm, kerb weight 144 kg, 17-inch alloys, tubeless tyres 80/100-17 front and 100/90-17 rear, front 240 mm disc, rear 130 mm drum, tank 12 L. — [Autocar India](https://www.autocarindia.com/bikes/bajaj/pulsar-ns125/specifications), [Rushlane](https://www.rushlane.com/bajaj-pulsar-ns-125-first-look-walkaround-exhaust-note-12400400.html)
- Bajaj's international pages are reported to list slightly different figures (ground clearance 178 mm, weight 141 kg); one comparison site lists 90/90-17 front and 120/80-17 rear. — [91wheels / carhp via search summary](https://www.91wheels.com/bike/bajaj/pulsar-ns-125/specifications)

### Inferences
- For the game model, 2012 x 810 x 1078 mm / 1353 mm wheelbase / ~144 kg are plausible for the Rouser but are not verified for the Philippine unit. The Philippine tank is 10.5 L against the Indian 12 L, so the tank shape or capacity may differ.
- Conversion check: 82.1 in = 2085 mm, 57.3 in = 1455 mm, 32.5 in = 826 mm (matches MotorcycleDB metric figures).

### Gaps
- No Kawasaki PH figures for Rouser length, width, height, wheelbase, seat height, ground clearance, weight, or tyre sizes. ZigWheels and Carmudi spec tables were blank for these.
- Rouser wheel type (alloy vs spoke) is not confirmed by a Rouser source.

## 3. Wheels, tyres, brakes, suspension, exhaust and engine appearance

### Takeaway
H2: five-spoke cast aluminium wheels, big Brembo Stylema monobloc calipers on dual 330 mm discs, a 43 mm inverted KYB fork (colour not sourced), a single-sided swingarm with an Ohlins shock, a larger right-side street muffler tucked close, and a supercharged inline-four. Rouser: front disc, rear drum, telescopic fork, rear shock, a 125 cc engine; component finish is not documented.

### Cited Findings
- H2: five-spoke cast aluminium wheels, street tyres 120/70 ZR17 and 200/55 ZR17, Brembo monobloc calipers with dual 330 mm front discs and a 250 mm rear, a 43 mm KYB fork, gas-charged rear shock, street exhaust larger and quieter than the H2R's with a bigger right-side muffler. — [Cycle News](https://www.cyclenews.com/2014/11/article/first-look-2015-kawasaki-ninja-h2-street/)
- H2 exhaust is stainless steel (H2R: titanium); H2 intake is a single plastic system (H2R: two carbon runners). — [Visordown](https://www.visordown.com/reviews/first-ride/first-ride-kawasaki-ninja-h2-and-h2r-review)
- H2 2019 update: LED lighting, self-healing top coat, lighter Brembo Stylema calipers, TFT dash. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawasaki_Ninja_H2)
- H2 engine: 998 cc DOHC liquid-cooled inline-four with supercharger and sealed chain final drive. — [Kawasaki USA 2025 H2 spec sheet](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs)
- Rouser: front disc brake (240 mm ventilated per Inside Racing, 2020), rear drum, telescopic fork, rear swingarm, chain drive. — [Inside Racing 2020](https://insideracing.com.ph/?p=49250), [ZigWheels PH specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi/specifications)
- Pulsar NS125 proxy: 17-inch bronze-coloured alloy wheels, Nitrox mono-shock, telescopic fork, perimeter frame. — [Rushlane](https://www.rushlane.com/bajaj-pulsar-ns-125-first-look-walkaround-exhaust-note-12400400.html)

### Inferences
- The Rouser's wheels, if shared with the Pulsar NS125, would be 17-inch alloys (Indian ones bronze). The Philippine colour is not confirmed.
- The H2's huge single-sided swingarm and right-side muffler dominate the right rear view; the left side shows the chain, so asymmetry matters in the model.

### Gaps
- Not found: caliper and fork colour, rim colour, and engine cover finish for either bike. The supercharger visibility/cover shape was not described in any fetched source.
- Not found: Rouser muffler shape and finish, and whether the Rouser has a rear monoshock or twin shocks; ZigWheels only lists "rear swingarm" and captions "Rear Suspension" and "Silencer" images without descriptions.

## 4. Instrument cluster, handlebar, mirrors, footpegs, controls

### Takeaway
H2: full TFT colour dash, clip-ons, mirrors on the fairing. Rouser: semi-digital cluster (analog tachometer + digital LCD), clip-on style bar, small visor.

### Cited Findings
- H2: all-digital TFT colour instrumentation (2025); digital dash shows gear, speed, traction control light (older review); LED lighting; smartphone connection via Rideology. — [Kawasaki USA 2025 H2 spec sheet](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs), [Visordown](https://www.visordown.com/reviews/first-ride/first-ride-kawasaki-ninja-h2-and-h2r-review)
- H2 has Ohlins electronic steering damper and quickshifter (2025). — [Kawasaki USA 2025 H2 spec sheet](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs)
- Rouser: semi-digital panel with analog tacho and digital display, FI indicator (2020); digital odometer, digital fuel gauge, side-stand indicator, engine check warning (current). — [Top Gear PH 2020](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2020-kawasaki-ns125-fi-a959-20200622), [ZigWheels PH specs](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi/specifications)
- Rouser has clip-on handlebar and a small visor carried over from the NS160/NS200 look. — [ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi)
- Rouser headlight turns on automatically (AHO, DC system). — [Inside Racing 2020](https://insideracing.com.ph/?p=49250)
- Pulsar NS125 Standard (India): digi-analogue console, halogen headlamp. — [Autocar India](https://www.autocarindia.com/bikes/bajaj/pulsar-ns125/specifications)

### Inferences
- The "clip-on" label for the Rouser likely means a low-mounted semi-raised bar on a naked bike, as on Pulsar NS models; this is not verified and needs photo reference.

### Gaps
- No sourced description of footpeg, mirror shape, switchgear or lever styling for either bike.

## 6. Distinctive features a modeller must get right

### Takeaway
H2: mirror-silver-black bodywork, bright green trellis frame (exposed ahead of and below the fairing), single central headlight, single-sided swingarm with large right muffler, 200-section rear tyre, supercharged engine. Rouser: compact naked Pulsar-NS-style body, twin pilot headlamps, split seat and grab rail, Black or Yellow.

### Cited Findings
- H2: green trellis frame, mono-headlight, mirror paint, single-sided swingarm, mirrors in place of H2R winglets, plastic panels in place of carbon. — [Visordown](https://www.visordown.com/reviews/first-ride/first-ride-kawasaki-ninja-h2-and-h2r-review), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawasaki_Ninja_H2)
- H2 tail unit adjusts into three positions to alter the side bolsters. — [Visordown](https://www.visordown.com/reviews/first-ride/first-ride-kawasaki-ninja-h2-and-h2r-review)
- Rouser: twin-pilot headlamps, split seat, split rear grab rail. — [Global Bajaj PH](https://philippines.globalbajaj.com/en/Brands/Rouser/Rouser-NS125-FI), [Rushlane](https://www.rushlane.com/bajaj-pulsar-ns-125-first-look-walkaround-exhaust-note-12400400.html)

### Inferences
- Variant contrast for the game: H2 base is the standard-trim bike (Spark Black); Carbon adds matte black with green accents and a carbon upper cowl; H2R adds winglets, wider body (33.5 in vs 30.3 in) and slicks; H2 SX is a different, touring-oriented body. Wikipedia describes the 2017 Carbon as a 120-unit limited edition with a carbon upper cowl, but the 2025 Carbon is a regular catalogue item, so the cowl detail may not carry over.

### Gaps
- Exact fairing intake shapes, belly pan, supercharger inlet duct position and tail lamp shape not sourced.

## 7. Model-year differences that change the look

### Takeaway
H2 has changed little visually since 2015; the 2019 update brought LED lights, a TFT dash and Stylema calipers. Rouser NS125 Fi: launched 2020 and listed with the same two colours through 2026; the price rose from P63,000 to P82,000.

### Cited Findings
- H2 launched 2015; updated standard H2 and limited Carbon edition (120 units, numbered) in 2017; H2 SX in 2018; H2 updated in 2019 with LED lighting, a self-healing top coat, Stylema calipers and TFT dash. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kawasaki_Ninja_H2)
- 2025 and 2026 US H2 Mirror Coated Spark Black unchanged. — [Kawasaki USA 2025 H2 spec sheet](https://www.kawasaki.com/en-us/products/ProductSpecSheetPDF/2025-ninja-h2-abs), [Total Motorcycle](https://www.totalmotorcycle.com/new-2026-kawasaki-performance-bikes-tmw-ux-site-upgrade)
- Rouser NS125 Fi: debuted in the Philippines in 2020 at P63,000; now P82,000; colours Black/Yellow in both periods. — [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2020-kawasaki-ns125-fi-a959-20200622), [ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi)
- Kawasaki added new paint schemes and graphics to the Rouser NS125 Fi on top of the NS-family styling. — [ZigWheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/kawasaki/rouser-ns125fi)

### Inferences
- The Rouser NS125 Fi seems unchanged in body since 2020; only paint/graphics refreshes are likely, but none were documented.
- Use the 2025/2026 H2 (Mirror Coated Spark Black, TFT, LED) as the model reference rather than the 2015 launch bike.

### Gaps
- No documented 2021 to 2026 graphic changes for the Rouser NS125 Fi. No confirmation of the Philippine H2 base-trim availability versus the Carbon-only listing.
- Kawasaki Motors Philippines' own pages were not accessible; verify on kawasaki.com.ph or a dealer.
