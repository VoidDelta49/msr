# Yamaha NMAX 155 and Yamaha XMAX 300: real-world visual appearance (Philippine market focus)

Research date: 2026-11-06. Method note: I could only read page text (no image viewing), so styling descriptions come from article text. Items under "Inferences" are modeller-oriented deductions, including hex colours, which are NOT sourced. Official yamaha-motor.com.ph pages could not be fetched (404 / no product links), so Philippine data comes from dealer/press aggregators (MotoDeal PH, Zigwheels PH, Carmudi, Top Gear PH, Motopinas, Manila Bulletin). Yamaha Europe pages 404'd, so EU data for the XMAX comes from secondary sites (autoevolution, motofichas).

Naming caution: in the Philippines the 2025 NMAX is sold as "NMAX" (Standard) and "NMAX Tech MAX". I found NO Philippine source for a "Connected" or "ABS"-suffixed variant: ABS and Y-Connect are standard on both (see Q7). "NMAX Connected" is the Indonesian/Argentine naming for the previous generation.

## Q1. Overall silhouette and body design language (front apron, screen, lights, tail, panels, floor, seat, grab rail)

### Takeaway
The 2025 "all-new" NMAX keeps the previous NMAX silhouette (compact, tall-ish step-through, 13-inch wheels) but gets a redesigned front clip (upward crest in the middle, projector LED headlights) and a new slimmer tail light replacing the old butterfly-shaped rear and boxy end. The XMAX 300 is the bigger, more aggressive sport-GT maxi-scooter with Yamaha's "X-design" front and rear fascia, an adjustable (now electric) windscreen and 15/14-inch wheels; the 2025 update was minor in the Philippines (new cluster, electric screen, reshaped nose).

### Cited Findings
**NMAX 155 (2025 model year, Philippine launch 15 Mar 2025)**
- Front clip is redesigned with new-look headlights and "an upward crest in the middle"; the rear drops the older butterfly-style taillights and boxy end for a new design that matches the front; overall silhouette kept, "so it still reads as an NMax" — [Top Gear PH, 2025 NMAX Tech MAX specs/prices](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2025-yamaha-nmax-tech-max-specs-prices-a5361-20250315)
- "Updated projector-style LED headlights and taillights"; two-level seat; 25-L underseat storage; the Tech MAX differs from base mainly by gold-coloured appliques on the wheels and gold seat stitching — [Carmudi, NMAX deliveries](https://carmudi.com.ph/journal/yamaha-motor-ph-starts-all-new-nmax-deliveries-to-clients); same details in [Zigwheels PH, NMAX turnover](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/yamaha-motor-ph-turns-over-all-new-nmax-initial-units-to-owners)
- First-ride review: "full LED lighting system with dual projectors"; new tail-light layout "slimmer than the previous generation's"; gold-coloured cast wheels; colour stitching on the seat; overall look "resembles the latest XMax" with finer details giving "a more elegant flair"; two-level seat and footboards (cramped for riders over 6 ft) — [Zigwheels PH, First ride: NMax Tech Max](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-expert-reviews/first-ride-yamaha-nmax-tech-max)
- Multiple front pockets, smart key (keyless), fuel tank about 7.1 L — [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2025-yamaha-nmax-tech-max-specs-prices-a5361-20250315); [Carmudi](https://carmudi.com.ph/journal/yamaha-motor-ph-starts-all-new-nmax-deliveries-to-clients)
- Top Gear PH's earlier preview used Yamaha Indonesia photos and warned local units may not look identical — [Top Gear PH preview](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/yamaha-nmax-2025-ph-preview-a5361-20250310)
- Philippine units are described as made in the Philippines — [Manila Bulletin via search snippet](https://mb.com.ph/2025/3/26/yamaha-nmax-tech-max-is-now-available) (page itself returned 403; claim taken from search-result summary, treat as secondary)

**XMAX 300 (2025 PH update; platform launched in PH March 2018)**
- 2025 PH XMAX "keeps the X-design on the front and rear fascia" — [Carmudi, Refreshed Yamaha XMAX now in PH, 13 May 2025](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/refreshed-yamaha-xmax-now-in-ph); [Zigwheels PH, 5 May 2025](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/yamaha-motor-ph-quietly-brings-in-updated-xmax) ("X-motif... aggressive look", otherwise similar to predecessor)
- 2018 PH launch description: sits between TMAX and NMAX in the Max range; adjustable windshield and handlebar; full LED lighting with DRL front and rear; keyless smart key; electric power socket; underseat storage for two full-face helmets — [Motopinas, 9 Mar 2018](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/yamaha-ph-launches-the-xmax-300.html)
- Europe 2025 update (not PH-confirmed): fairing nose reshaped for a more distinctive look and less buffeting; dual LED headlight; high-mounted integrated front turn signals; electrically adjustable windscreen, 95 mm vertical travel, switch on left handlebar; restyled dashboard under a smoked screen; Tech variants get special emblems on the bodywork — [autoevolution, 2025 Max scooter refresh](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/yamaha-s-smash-hit-max-sport-scooters-get-2025-refresh-and-upgrades-245714.html)
- Electric windscreen has aluminium brackets (EU Tech MAX) — [Motofichas](https://www.motofichas.com/marcas/yamaha/xmax-300-tech-max)

### Inferences
- (Background knowledge, not sourced here; verify against photos.) NMAX is a short-wheelbase "urban" scooter: tall front apron with a V/crest nose, split two-level seat (rider seat lower in front, raised pillion/backrest-like rear section), a wide step-through floorboard with a short tunnel, and a rear grab-rail/rack integrated into the tail. XMAX is longer/lower/wider with a distinct tunnel, larger floorboards, a taller adjustable screen, and a two-piece "X" shaped front face (upper headlight cluster with lower air-intake/apron), plus an "X" tail-light signature.
- The 2025 NMAX adopts the XMAX-style sharp, horizontal, slim lighting language (Zigwheels says it "resembles the latest XMax"), so for the game both models should share a family resemblance with the XMAX being clearly larger.

### Gaps
- No text source gave a full panel-by-panel description (side panels, grab rail, mirror shape, tail lamp pattern) for either bike; need reference photos.
- Official Yamaha Motor Philippines product pages were unreachable, so no official design copy was obtained.
- Whether the Philippine XMAX has the EU 2025 "dual LED headlight / high-mounted turn signal" nose is not stated in PH sources (Carmudi says only the X-design is retained).

## Q2. Key dimensions (length, width, height, wheelbase, seat height, ground clearance, wheel/tire sizes, weight)

NMAX 155 (2025): 1935 x 740 x 1200 mm, seat 770 mm, wheelbase about 1340 mm, 13-inch wheels F/R. XMAX 300: 2180 x 795 x 1410 (screen low) to 1460 (screen high) mm, wheelbase 1540 mm, seat 795 mm, 135 mm ground clearance, about 181 kg wet (PH), 15-inch front and 14-inch rear.

### Inferences
- NMAX 13/13-inch vs XMAX 15/14-inch: the XMAX front wheel is visibly larger than the rear; on the NMAX both are equal and small. Width difference is only 55 mm but the XMAX is 245 mm longer, 210 mm taller (screen down) and has 200 mm more wheelbase, so proportions differ mostly in length and height.
- Screen travel: 1460 - 1410 = 50 mm overall height variation in the PH spec vs 95 mm screen travel in EU copy; treat 1410 as screen down.

### Gaps
- Official NMAX wheelbase, rear tire section and weight for the 2025 PH model not confirmed from an official PH sheet (figures come from Indonesian/EU aggregators and a possibly older PH FAQ).
- No PH-sourced wheel rim widths or exact disc diameters for NMAX.

## Q3. Wheel and tire look, brakes, ABS ring, fork / rear shock, exhaust / muffler

### Takeaway
NMAX: small 13-inch cast wheels, Tech MAX with gold-coloured appliques/cast wheels, disc brakes front and rear with ABS. XMAX: 15" front / 14" rear cast wheels, 267 mm front disc with 2-piston caliper and 245 mm rear disc, telescopic fork and unit-swing rear shock(s). Exhaust finish is poorly documented in text.

### Cited Findings
- NMAX (MotoDeal PH): disc brakes front and rear, ABS; telescopic front forks and unit-swing rear suspension — [MotoDeal PH NMAX 155](https://www.motodeal.com.ph/motorcycles/yamaha/nmax-155)
- NMAX Tech MAX: "gold-coloured cast wheels" / "gold-coloured appliques on the wheels" — [Zigwheels PH first ride](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-expert-reviews/first-ride-yamaha-nmax-tech-max); [Carmudi](https://carmudi.com.ph/journal/yamaha-motor-ph-starts-all-new-nmax-deliveries-to-clients)
- NMAX wheel size: 13 in front and rear (tire sizes 110/70 R13 and 130/70 R13) — [Zigwheels PH FAQ](https://www.zigwheels.ph/new-motorcycles/yamaha/nmax/faq) (see Q2 caveat)
- XMAX PH 2025: telescopic front fork, rear unit-swing suspension, hydraulic single disc brakes (front) with ABS and traction control; V-belt CVT — [Carmudi](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/refreshed-yamaha-xmax-now-in-ph)
- XMAX EU Tech MAX: front disc 267 mm with 2-piston caliper, rear disc 245 mm; wheels 15 in / 14 in — [Motofichas](https://www.motofichas.com/marcas/yamaha/xmax-300-tech-max). (Carmudi describes PH as "single disc brakes"; Motofichas shows rear disc, so "single" probably means single front disc; flag as uncertain.)
- XMAX Tech variants (EU): aluminium footplates, mirrors and grips — [autoevolution](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/yamaha-s-smash-hit-max-sport-scooters-get-2025-refresh-and-upgrades-245714.html) (not confirmed for PH standard model)

### Inferences
- (Unsourced background, low confidence; confirm with photos.) Both bikes use multi-spoke cast alloy wheels, a disc at each end with an ABS tone ring on the hub, a conventional (right-way-up) telescopic fork, and a unit-swing rear with exposed-spring shock(s). Shock count (single vs twin) and exact spoke pattern are NOT confirmed by any source I read.
- Exhaust: both have a side-mounted, right-side muffler with a short chromed/black heat-shield cover and a stubby tail tip hugging the swingarm (typical Yamaha Blue Core scooters). Not text-sourced.

### Gaps
- No source describing rim colour (black vs silver) for the Standard NMAX or XMAX, rim spoke count, ABS ring visibility, fork stanchion size/finish, shock spring colour, or muffler finish. Reference photos needed.
- No confirmation of NMAX front/rear disc diameters.

## Q4. Instrument cluster, handlebar, mirrors and controls

### Takeaway
NMAX Standard has a full-digital speedometer; NMAX Tech MAX adds a colour 4.2-inch TFT with Garmin StreetCross navigation, Y-Connect and notifications. XMAX 2025 PH has a new 4.2-inch TFT plus a 3.2-inch LCD, improved backlighting, Garmin StreetCross navigation, USB-C socket, electronic windscreen.

### Cited Findings
- NMAX Tech MAX cluster (PH): two small digital displays; upper shows speedometer, odometer, fuel gauge; lower switches between rev meter, trip info and Garmin-powered navigation; Y-Connect smartphone pairing — [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2025-yamaha-nmax-tech-max-specs-prices-a5361-20250315); [MotoDeal PH](https://www.motodeal.com.ph/motorcycles/yamaha/nmax-155)
- NMAX: "full digital speedometer"; 4.2-inch colour TFT (Tech MAX only) with onboard navigation via Garmin StreetCross and smartphone notifications — [Carmudi](https://carmudi.com.ph/journal/yamaha-motor-ph-starts-all-new-nmax-deliveries-to-clients)
- NMAX Standard lacks the Y-ECVT and the new instrument cluster of the Tech MAX but has the new body design — [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/moto-sapiens/motorcycle-news/2025-yamaha-nmax-tech-max-specs-prices-a5361-20250315). (MotoDeal PH conversely says Standard price includes Y-Connect: dealer-listing wording, unclear.)
- XMAX PH 2025: new instrument panel with 4.2-inch TFT and 3.2-inch LCD, improved backlighting; Garmin StreetCross navigation (phone-based); electronically adjustable windscreen; USB Type-C; modifiable handlebar — [Carmudi](https://www.carmudi.com.ph/journal/refreshed-yamaha-xmax-now-in-ph); [Zigwheels PH](https://www.zigwheels.ph/motorcycle-news/yamaha-motor-ph-quietly-brings-in-updated-xmax)
- XMAX EU: 4.2-inch TFT plus backlit LCD for speed and trip distance, both under a smoked screen; TFT shows rev counter, clock, trip computer, fuel, temperatures, service reminder; Bluetooth smartphone connectivity — [autoevolution](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/yamaha-s-smash-hit-max-sport-scooters-get-2025-refresh-and-upgrades-245714.html); [Motofichas](https://www.motofichas.com/marcas/yamaha/xmax-300-tech-max)
- XMAX: smart key unlocks seat, fuel flap and storage by button; two extra front storage compartments including a USB-C socket (EU) — [Motopinas 2018](https://www.motopinas.com/motorcycle-news/yamaha-ph-launches-the-xmax-300.html); [autoevolution](https://www.autoevolution.com/news/yamaha-s-smash-hit-max-sport-scooters-get-2025-refresh-and-upgrades-245714.html)

### Inferences
- Both cluster layouts are a two-tier arrangement: a small LCD strip above (speed/trip) and a larger TFT below, under a smoked cover. For the game, model the XMAX cluster as a wide, low, dark-smoked visor with a rectangular 4.2" TFT; the NMAX Tech MAX cluster is similar but narrower.
- (Unsourced) Mirrors are angular, stalk-mounted on the fairing top with integrated or separate turn signals; XMAX 2025 EU moved turn signals up high near the mirrors/screen ("high-mounted integrated front turn signals").

### Gaps
- Mirror shape, switch-gear layout, grip finish: no text source. Whether the PH NMAX Standard retains a monochrome LCD vs colour TFT is described inconsistently ("full digital speedometer").
- Cluster hardware conflict: Top Gear PH/MotoDeal say "two small digital displays" for Tech MAX; Carmudi/Zigwheels say "4.2-inch colour TFT". Likely the same hardware (a TFT plus a small LCD, mirroring the XMAX), but not explicitly confirmed.
